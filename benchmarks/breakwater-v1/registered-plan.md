# Breakwater v1 against Diamond Port 1.8.28

## Hypothesis

A power-0.1 bullet can intercept an inferred incoming bullet. At ordinary duel range Diamond often spends 1.95 energy on that shot. If Breakwater can induce a predictable incoming ray, it can spend less energy on defense, avoid damage and deny the shooter its hit bonus. It still needs survival or offensive points. While the gun is free, Breakwater takes selective attacks on targets whose stationary and constant-motion forecasts agree, or attempts to finish a low-energy target. When interception is infeasible, it evades. A miss against a stationary or slowly moving defender could be particularly costly.

The pinned Python gun attempts a previously aimed shot, gates firing on cool/aligned state and varies power with distance, energy and rammer classification. Its surfer compares stop and two orbit directions and can anticipate incoming fire before seeing an energy drop. The server checks intersecting bullet segments in the same tick. These facts permit the proposed tactic. They do not establish that inferred shots have the right origin, timing or direction for interception. ## Fixed controller and evidence checks

At range ≥350 with a scan no older than one tick, brake and face tangent to the opponent. Each round draws one seeded lateral side. Four sidestep command ticks at target speed 4 give the outgoing shield a crossing angle. The search spans at most 90 future launch ticks and 90 collision ticks per launch, breaks at body arrival and projects the pinned move-then-turn motion. It uses a shield power of 0.1, at least 2 px lateral displacement, radius 18 and the actual current gun heading for immediate fire. A confirmed shield still leaves an emergency evasion window when its target wave approaches within five ticks. Wall-safe escape tries both lateral directions and then the center over eight projected steps.

Require adjacent scans and own position history. Exclude observed body contact and either scan within 26 px of a wall. Apply confirmed damage and hit bonuses at their verified visibility tick. Accept an energy residual in [0.1, 3] only after initial cooling and outside the inferred heat lockout. Mask minimum-power drops once the configured inactivity threshold can have elapsed. This can miss real minimum shots, and simultaneous decay can still bias other inferred powers. Learn a signed heading residual only from a unique wave match on owner, power, age and ray geometry. Average the latest eight residuals clipped to ±12 degrees. The attack selector retains the latest 32 jointly resolved stopped/constant-motion passage observations from confirmed attack shots. It chooses the more frequently successful ray, with constant motion winning ties. A forecast must put both rays inside both projected footprints unless the opponent has ≤4 energy. Offensive power is 1, capped for finishing. The controller reserves known unresolved threats; future enemy shots remain estimates.

Each game must yield 20 ordered, sealed round rows with no unclassified shot. Own round-win flags must sum to official first places. Match every additive endpoint counter to the rows and verify the retained ordered model history against its summary. Count unique confirmed shield IDs colliding with the opponent's projectile. Both a projectile collision and a bot hit can legally occur for one bullet during the same engine tick.

## Reproduction

On the registered Ubuntu 26.04 x86_64 image, use Java 17 for both Gradle builds, `./gradlew --no-daemon clean build` and `./gradlew -p experiments/Breakwater --no-daemon clean build`. The build workflow tests the released JAR's callbacks. Compare committed and rebuilt entries with `tools/verify_jar.py`; launch the committed, hash-verified artifact. Run `python -m unittest discover -s tools -p 'test_breakwater_experiment.py'`. The registered acquisition command under JDK 25 and Python 3.13 is `python tools/breakwater_experiment.py --output build/breakwater-v1`. The dedicated measurement workflow runs this command once on independent clearance of the frozen head. The embedded server chooses a headless port on an isolated hosted VM. The existing interactive server is external to that VM.
