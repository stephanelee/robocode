# Independent sequence-predictor prior-art inventory

2026-11-04. Read-only inspection; no constants chosen, model fitted, trajectory reconstructed, diagnostic or game run. Scope: current AIS/Challenger/Breakwater/Meridian/Peak sources and research docs, the off-mainline `codex/meridian-motion-memory` and `codex/meridian-prior` work, supervised forecast tools/model locks, and literal concept searches across all82 locally available origin remote refs. This supports a repository-specific finding, not a proof of absence in unfetched/deleted work or novelty in Robocode generally.

## Conclusion

I found **no implemented or measured own-bot predictor that matches a complete ordered recent consecutive-scan motion prefix against past prefixes, then retrieves their already-complete observed continuation trajectories from a bounded dense scan-history bank**. That is materially different from adding two lagged velocities to crossing KNN and from the supervised residual/containment models already rejected here.

Do not call historical-path retrieval generally novel: the pinned Adaptive Prime opponent already replays observed target-history continuations, and pattern matching is established Robocode terminology. Its source is relevant design prior art, though I found no own-bot live/diagnostic experiment isolating a true multi-step prefix match plus complete continuation retrieval. A generic “use movement history/KNN” proposal would be too broad and would conceal substantial negative evidence.

## Opponent prior art matters

Pinned Adaptive Prime `bot_core/gun/guns/displacement/gun.py` already retrieves historical target paths from `TargetHistoryStore`. Source is retained under Peak's staged opponents at the pinned67b68c22 commit. It ranks historical anchor indices using current-versus-past heading/speed/lateral/advancing/wall context plus one-step heading-change distance, replays subsequent observed displacements at current bullet speed, and combines candidate bearings by angular density. The shared history stores bounded observed positions and can have nonconsecutive observed turns.

That is genuine historical continuation replay, so a proposal to “retrieve prior observed continuations instead of a point mean” alone is not new even within the inspected code ecosystem. The narrower untested own-bot distinction is **matching the ordered multi-scan prefix itself** and requiring an already-complete consecutive-scan suffix before admission, rather than matching one anchor plus a derivative or learning one crossing offset. This inventory does not claim that distinction will improve predictions or beats the opponent's implementation.

## Conditions for a distinct next question, without selecting an algorithm

A potentially distinct question is whether a bounded, legal, completed-prefix/suffix motion library gives a useful continuation forecast beyond the existing scalar-crossing/current-summary baselines. Define an actual sequence representation and retrieval target prospectively before code: what constitutes a consecutive scan, how a prefix ends, when its suffix becomes complete/admissible, and how current geometry maps a stored continuation. “Sequence” cannot merely rename a small lag feature vector while retaining the same scalar GF bank.

The central leakage constraint is strict availability: every stored suffix must finish and be legally observed before the current query, with deterministic same-tick callback/revision order. A historical anchor preceding the query is insufficient if its suffix extends into the query's future. Do not train/retrieve overlapping current suffixes just because the eventual log contains them. Missing scans and round/target resets must break continuity; unknown terminal suffixes remain excluded with counted masks, not misses. Long overlapping dense windows are highly correlated and cannot be counted as independent evidence.

A translation/rotation or mirrored replay needs explicit semantics: signed velocity and heading wrap, walls/legal bounds, turn/acceleration constraints, and bullet creation/flight timing can make a geometrically transformed observed path unrealizable in the current arena. Replaying a path is a forecast, not an engine intervention. Do not silently clip it into a different path or fill gaps with future interpolation. Prior/current own-shot context may matter because Diamond reacts to firing; target motion history alone is not a causal response simulator.

The existing45-round supervised corpus is mostly Retcon and official samples, with many already-reused train/dev outcomes and spent withheld/fresh transfer games. It cannot become fresh Diamond confirmation merely by making dense windows. Peak recordings provide legal scans but do not automatically prove every policy-query/model-revision ordering required for a live sequence library. An offline library built from them must have explicitly retrospective/development scope and fixed whole-game roles; no live improvement follows from an in-sample best-match or hindsight candidate union.

Recommendation: this narrower completed-sequence retrieval family appears untested in the inspected own-bot studies, unlike the duplicate continuation controller and four-predictor guard. It is reasonable to investigate a concrete prospective diagnostic design next, while citing the opponent displacement replay and all rejected history/forecast variants. No constants, representation, fitting, data reuse, implementation or game budget is authorized by this inventory. The new design should first establish legal availability, physical/label semantics and honest comparison scope rather than claim novelty or competitive strength.
