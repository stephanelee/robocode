# Meridian gun research inventory: common-cohort arbitration is already prior art

2026-11-04. Read-only inventory and source/schema feasibility assessment. No code/tool/protocol/freeze edits, benchmarks, diagnostics, prediction reconstruction, replay, fits, parameter choices or games were run for this note. The deferred Branch draft remains deferred and unregistered. This note authorizes no candidate or acquisition.

## Recommendation

**Reject “replace unconditional supported-GF override with shared-outcome expert arbitration” as a novel, immediately justified next live experiment.** It is a concrete limitation of original Meridian v3, but the same essential intervention was already implemented and tested in DiamondChallenger PR64: freeze the learned ray plus three kinematic rays ex ante, create cohorts only on matching confirmed shots, compare all four on shared outcomes/denominators, and require an advantage over the three before overriding. Its valid20-round pilot failed. AISurvivor's existing DuelGun and the pinned opponents also provide earlier virtual-gun selection prior art. Different Meridian predictor features, legacy score semantics and movement make an exact Meridian implementation different, but do not establish that reapplying the mechanism would help.

A narrower meaningful next **research question**, if root wants it separately defined and independently reviewed, is: *Can the existing Peak archive reproduce each supported confirmed launch's four predictions strictly from information available before that launch, with the reconstructed learner/selection history reconciling to recorded states; and, only where reconstruction is identifiable, how do their geometric errors compare on one common later legal crossing?* This would be a descriptive feasibility/representation check on spent data, not a selector fit or competitive test. First determine reconstructibility; missing or ambiguous predictions stay unknown. No margin/window/smoothing/support/acceptance constants are selected here. New live telemetry is not automatically required just to answer a narrower historical question, but the archive is not already a directly recorded four-expert cohort dataset.

## Source assessment: the proposed gun limitation is real but familiar

Original v1/v2 `Meridian.gun` computes three immutable forecast angles from stopped, constant-heading/signed-speed and observed-turn-rate motion. It grades all three together every fourth fresh-scan decision at the then-selected expert's predicted impact turn, using a later exact-turn scan and smooth chord-miss score. These trials need not correspond to fired shots. The three scores update with the same fixed decay and select the largest; none is a calibrated actual-hit probability.

V3 preserves that selector, then obtains `estimate=crossingGun.aim(launch)`, sets `learned=Double.isFinite(estimate)` and `aim=learned?estimate:aims[best]`. The learned query requires at least8 retained examples and effective context weight>=4; the192-bank context is absolute lateral speed, radial speed, forward wall clearance and flight time, voting on51 GF grid points. This support is coverage of similar examples, not demonstrated accuracy relative to the kinematic forecasts. Once finite, GF overrides regardless of legacy scores. The three legacy scores never grade GF. That is exactly the proposed capability gap.

V4 changes power only and retains this override. V5 restores v3 power and the same live online gun; it changes defense, not arbitration. V6 is a different experiment: it removes the legacy trial machinery from the runtime gun, loads a frozen206-row bank, bypasses the support gate and does not collect online crossing updates. Although its stacked branch still contains `SurfPlan.java`, v6 `Meridian.run` uses the restored v3 route, not SurfPlan; file presence alone is not execution. It supplies no expert-arbitration test.

### Other relevant selection prior art, with different evidential scope

Production `DuelGun` already freezes a real GF/current candidate alongside head-on/linear/circular at matching confirmed origin/power and scores joint resolved virtual cohorts. `docs/coherent-duel-v2.md`/PR43 restored a genuine GF/circular hint and finishing behavior; its168-round bundled Retcon/self/sample comparison passed. That is positive in-roster evidence for a combined product revision, **not** proof of isolated arbitration benefit against Diamond or permission to transfer production code blindly.

Meridian v1's three-expert selector itself derives from an established virtual-gun idea. Pinned Retcon chooses head-on/linear by cumulative virtual hit rate; Diamond's virtual-gun manager compares predicted rays on firing waves with a smooth angular-width-normalized score. Their source audits in `docs/opponents/retcon.md` and `docs/opponents/diamond-port.md` show precedent, not a causal counter-strategy. Neither a different smoothing score nor the phrase “calibrate GF” makes this a new statistical or competitive mechanism. Shared-cohort success rates/smooth errors on actual chosen trajectories are conditional proxies; they are not calibrated counterfactual hit probabilities.

## Can existing Peak evidence support a leakage-free descriptive check?

### What is directly present

`PeakTelemetry.Models` seals the current incoming histogram, bounded CrossingGun examples/count/next/pending launches, gunSkill and pending virtual trials. `labels.jsonl` contains ordered crossing rows with round,observed turn, full Launch(turn,origin,power,bearing,sign,range,context), current/previous legal target poses, reason and offset; trial rows contain observed turn,SCORED/MISSING_SCAN, Trial(due,three frozen angles,origin) and observed target pose. Cold state fixes an empty bank and initial skill. Markers give actual confirmed fire turn/power plus learned/fallback requests, but aim markers have no decision turn or angle. Official raw recordings retain exact own confirmed shot IDs/origin/power/direction and the ScannedBotEvents delivered to the observer, including public owner IDs.

From the earlier all-round audit, Peak Diamond has197 confirmed shots,191 resolved crossings and six round-terminal pending launches;zero crossing/trial masks. Those counts show a potentially useful archive, not that all197 four-expert forecasts are identified. Retcon/AIS/Walls archives are separate cold games and should not be omitted if a future descriptive scope includes transfer.

### What it does not directly contain

There is no ex-ante confirmed-shot record holding all four candidate angles, GF availability/support/model revision and selected expert at its request. A legacy Trial has no request/creation/decision ID or decision turn, excludes GF, is created only every fourth fresh tick and often exists without a shot. Its outcome time is the then-selected expert's forecast horizon, not necessarily the actual confirmed shot's first crossing. Joining it to a crossing merely by similar origin/due turn, or treating it as a fire cohort, is unsafe.

The actual fired heading is the current physical gun direction within an alignment tolerance, not necessarily the selected desired GF/kinematic angle. It cannot stand in for the missing desired prediction. End-of-round/final bank/skill include future outcomes, possibly the evaluated shot itself: recomputing a historical aim with those snapshots leaks future information. A full fitted206-bank from another game is a different model, not the deployed online query.

### A strictly limited forward reconstruction may be possible, but is not yet verified

1. Identify a confirmed launch solely by recorded round/turn/actual origin/power and the journal/pending launch metadata, with unique matching and masks for mismatch/absence. One launch per bot per tick is possible; do not infer a unique identity from equal-valued context alone. Decision turn is creation−1 under the frozen source's request contract.
2. Rebuild the original192 FIFO bank from cold state by processing crossing admissions in recorded callback order, retaining all masks. At query time use only labels that the source proves were admitted before that query. For a fresh-scan fire, `onScannedBot(t)` finishes crossing/trial updates before `scan.t=t` becomes available to monitored `gun(t)`; correctly ordered same-turn past-shot labels may therefore be legal. Do not mechanically substitute `labelTurn<decisionTurn` for actual source ordering, or include an uncertain same-turn update by guess. Reconcile every reconstructed count,next,example and round boundary to sealed DTOs before interpreting query outputs.
3. The Launch context/bearing/sign/power can determine the same GF query against that preceding bank. To recover the three kinematic predictions, the source needs the decision-time own origin, latest fresh legal target scan and consecutive-previous-scan turn rate. A confirmed firing request requires `scan.t==decisionTurn`, so archived own ScannedBotEvents are a plausible source, but delivery/identity/ordering and any boundary ambiguity must be demonstrated from source and retained evidence. Observer-only enemy state, gun direction or later motion must never substitute for unavailable bot observations. Replay only frozen predictors from their historical inputs, not a new selector or alternative movement policy.
4. Cold skill and ordered SCORED trial rows may allow forward reconstruction of the exact legacy best expert; require reconciliation to every sealed gunSkill snapshot and correct same-turn ordering. Do not infer ex-ante best from which forecast later misses least. Missing initial inputs, history, model transitions or identity remain unknown, not imputed from future scans.
5. Freeze all four resulting predictions and availability in the descriptive record before attaching the later common crossing label. A valid consecutive legal scan bracket supplies one shared actual first-far-edge/centre crossing for all available rays; it is a crossing-proximity proxy. Physical swept-ray intersection over an entire flight would require a different complete scan-sequence/missingness contract. Do not silently mix these objectives. Unsupported GF is unavailable,not a zero-error/success/miss; terminal pending and ambiguous/masked rows stay explicit for the common cohort.

These are **proposed feasibility requirements, not findings**: no reconstruction, replay or geometric scoring was executed here. The journals alone cannot certify a directly observed ex-ante cohort, but the combined journals+cold state+exact source+archived legal scan stream may support a leakage-free retrospective reconstruction. A separately specified read-only analysis could stop at “not identifiable” without acquiring more games. It would use spent development evidence and cannot validate a calibrated selector, estimate effect on firing opportunities or establish strength.

### What genuinely new telemetry would be needed for a future directly observed experiment

If a separately justified future study needs actual ex-ante cohorts rather than reconstruction, freeze one own pending candidate set before requesting fire: request/decision/target/launch identity, all three kinematic desired angles and horizons, the GF angle or explicit unavailability, current support/model-revision reference, current legacy scores/selected expert, origin/power and actual chosen desired versus physical heading. Confirm/reanchor only through matching actual own bullet creation; label all four on the same declared future legal observation contract with joint missing/terminal/ambiguous masks. Record cold and lossless model/revision/callback ordering and seal after the terminal drain; keep cumulative versus bounded support distinct. Do not select cohorts based on which expert later does well, treat removed bullets as known misses, mix global legacy and conditional GF denominators or rescore unresolved outcomes as losses.

That is a telemetry specification question, not permission to implement another guarded selector or choose its constants. Any change in selected desired angle can change alignment, confirmed firing times, future enemy movement, hits, energy and subsequent training; historical common crossing errors remain held-trajectory proxies. Even a convincing reconstructed GF-versus-expert difference would motivate further mechanistic reasoning, not automatically authorize a new live experiment already resembling PR64.

## Disposition

No old gameplay was re-audited here; report links retain source, raw evidence and independent reviews. No fresh constants or algorithms are chosen.

Do not register live “calibrated GF arbitration” as the next new mechanism on current evidence. The defensible optional next question is the narrow reconstructibility/common-label descriptive question above, prospectively scoped and independently reviewed before any calculation. If it is not worth that limited read-only cost, reject this direction too and continue source/prior-art research; do not spend fresh games merely to rediscover the shared-cohort idea.
