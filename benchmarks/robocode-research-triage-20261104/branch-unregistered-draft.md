## Design ambiguities resolved prospectively

1. “First relevant” cannot depend on an arbitrarily enumerated branch or a post-game bullet. Use one legal decision-time order defined below, common to all four plans. Existing inferred-wave insertion order breaks an otherwise exact tie; it is legal own history.
2. Merely leaving a later wave unpassed can permit a late-wall-only branch when that wave never encounters the held route within50. Eligibility therefore requires a later alive wave's radial annulus/body overlap in the already-computed held suffix. This is necessary radial overlap, **not** actual angular ray/body contact or known enemy aim. The branch may avoid that held encounter entirely.
3. “After clear” must not mean switching on the clearing tick. The first strict near-radius clear at step k permits an orbit flip only at step k+1; k50 cannot create a real flip. Target-ray wall death before or on that clear suppresses the branch. No-wave/one-wave and ineligible cases alias the held plan bit-for-bit.
4. A branch alias must not pay a hypothetical flip cost, mutate side, change ties or recalculate a slightly different floating result. It uses the exact held score/first command and an explicit no-actual-flip flag.
5. Later-wave eligibility is a held-suffix calculation, so it requires no fifth trajectory and no extra simulation beyond the two held plans and at most two branch suffixes. This is fixed bounded enumeration, not a search over switch times.

## Exact legal ordering and four plans

At decision turn t, first perform the unchanged Peak retirement. Take one immutable snapshot of the retained inferred waves, existing incoming learner and legal own/last-scan state. Each retained wave has a distinct round-local monotonically increasing insertion ordinal assigned exactly once when the unchanged inference creates it; equal-valued inferred waves still have different IDs, with no value-based deduplication. Ordinal is identity/tie bookkeeping, not a learned feature. No new wave is inferred during enumeration.

Legal input invariants are finite own/scan/wave fields, positive arena dimensions, inferred origins strictly inside the arena, power in[0.1,3], positive v, an already-inferred creation turn and nonnegative near(w,0). Reject a nonfinite/illegal state as a permanent invariant failure with no policy fallback, radius clamp, new retirement rule or inferred-origin repair. Interior origins, nonnegative radius and positive speed make a ray leaving the convex rectangle unable to re-enter as its radius grows. For wave w with speed v=20−3p, at forecast step i define `near(w,i)=(t+i−w.turn+1)*v` and `far=near+v`, including i0. Obtain the exact unchanged Peak weighted rays once. Use a separate eligibility-only i0 mask: a ray is i0-eligible only when its near point lies strictly inside `(0,width)×(0,height)`. For actual held scoring retain frozen Peak literally: initialize every future ray alive, and first perform its near-point wall-death test at step1. Do not replace those held flags with the i0 mask. At every future step advance near/far normally; remove a ray before that sweep if its near point reaches/outcrosses any boundary. Death is permanent within that plan; a far point outside alone does not kill it. Under the legal monotone-radius domain an i0-dead ray cannot re-enter, but held score parity is preserved by literal step1 behavior rather than relying on a reordered calculation. Future eligibility survival and radial tests use the held step1..50 flags; only decision-time condition(b) uses the separate i0 mask.

Compute the two HELD plans H− and H+ exactly as frozen Peak for all50 steps, including full per-ray peak, wall and first-sign-switch accounting. Cache poses/near-front values/alive flags needed for eligibility; the held final scores and first commands must equal Peak exactly for identical inputs. Caching is an implementation optimization, not permission to change arithmetic/order. It must not mutate any learner.

Let d0(w) be own-centre to inferred-origin distance at decision time. A wave is decision-relevant iff (a) `near(w,0)<=d0(w)+18`, (b) at least one ray is eligible under the separate i0 mask, and (c) at least one HELD path has some i in1..50 with an alive ray after that tick's wall-death test and radial overlap `near(w,i)<=distance(origin,heldPose_i)+18` AND `far(w,i)>=distance(origin,heldPose_i)−18`. This is an angular-agnostic necessary encounter window, not a collision label.

Order decision-relevant waves by ascending exact double `priority(w)=max(0,(d0(w)−18−far(w,0))/v)`, then by increasing wave insertion ordinal. No tolerance buckets, fitting, later reordering or predicted-hit probabilities. The target is the first in this order, common to all four plans. If fewer than two decision-relevant waves exist, both nominal branches alias their corresponding held plan exactly. All retained waves, including decision-irrelevant ones, still contribute their unchanged Peak risk; relevance gates branching only, never masks risk or retirement.

For each first sign s∈{−1,+1}, define nominal B_s from H_s:

- Search H_s in chronological order for the first k∈1..50 where target `near(target,k)>distance(target.origin,H_s.pose_k)+18` strictly. This is path-dependent. Equality does not clear. If no k, k50, or no target ray remains alive after the k wall test, B_s aliases H_s. Any one surviving target ray suffices; deaths of the other target rays do not suppress the branch. Monotone ray death makes the last condition exclude wall death before/on clear; wall death itself never creates a switch.
- Otherwise inspect only waves later than target in the fixed decision-relevant order. A later wave qualifies iff it has at least one ray alive after the wall test at k, its near radius at k is `<=distance(origin,H_s.pose_k)+18`, and there exists j∈{k+1,..,50} in the **held H_s suffix** with at least one ray alive after that tick's wall test and the two radial overlap inequalities above. Any surviving ray suffices at k and j; the requirement is not angular proximity to the bot, nor identification of a particular ray as the cause of radial overlap. Monotone death means any ray surviving at j also survived at k. No post-result angular refinement is permitted. Record the complete qualifying-wave ID set and its earliest qualifying j per wave. No qualifying later wave means B_s aliases H_s. This excludes flips justified solely by late wall costs after all modeled later encounters have ended or lie outside the horizon.
- If eligible, B_s shares H_s poses/actions/risks through k inclusive, then uses the unchanged `Flight.orbit` with sign −s for steps k+1..50. It has exactly one actual future flip; no target reselection, delayed switch search, second branch or changed opponent position forecast. Ray geometry/death is still the same legal model, and own two-pass physics is applied to each new suffix pose. Compute all retained waves' per-ray peaks across the **entire** two-leg path, not a sum of leg peaks, plus the unchanged wall cost over all50 ticks.

Exactly four nominal scores are exposed: H−,H+,B−,B+. The exact maximum is198 actual `PeakMotion.step` calls:100 held calls plus two suffixes of at most49; enforce a separate <=200 invariant guard. Counters count actual calls only, with no call charged for an already-cached prefix or a branch alias. Eligibility cache scans, ray-survival checks, radial-geometry operations and kernel evaluations have their own explicit counters, so they cannot hide a fifth trajectory or extra physics work. Never extend a plan to finish a wave. No candidate-dependent cutoff/timeout-based choice, action subsampling, alternate-path fallback or hidden extra rollout. If finite arithmetic, trace, bounded-cost or source invariants fail, fail the experiment rather than dispatch a different controller.

For any actual path, initial-switch cost is the original2 iff its first sign differs from committed side. For H−/H+ retain frozen Peak's literal arithmetic: initialize the scalar score to initial-switch cost, add all50 wall terms chronologically, then for each wave in retained insertion order sum its weighted ray peaks in existing ray order and add that wave scalar to the score. Never reconstruct held totals from diagnostic component sums. A real B_s uses that same order, with its own chronological50 wall terms: initialize initial-switch scalar, add each wall term for steps1..50 in order, add the actual future-flip scalar +2 exactly once **after all wall additions and before any wave-risk additions**, then add wave/ray risks in retained-wave/existing-ray order. Reusing a prefix must preserve that literal partial accumulator. Aliases copy the held score and command object values verbatim, add no future-flip cost, and perform no arithmetic recomputation. Independently summed diagnostic components may differ by rounding; validate their reconciliation with `abs(total−componentSum) <= 1e−9 + 1e−12*max(abs(total),abs(componentSum))`. This tolerance is validation only and never affects scores, eligibility, comparisons or ties. The risk kernel remains `40/(1+d2/500)` with centre-to-finite-sweep d2, max per ray over50 then unchanged weight sum per wave and sum all waves. Each wave stays bounded80+1e−9; ray mass tolerance remains1e−12, validation only. No clamp/renormalization or epsilon is used in policy comparison.

Rank by exact numeric score ascending; exact equal scores prefer no actual flip, then first sign−1, then the held nominal plan before its identical branch alias. This fixes every tie. Issue **only the selected plan's first command**, set committed side to its first sign as Peak does, and replan from live legal observations next turn. Do not precommit the future flip, schedule an actuator or remember an intended continuation as a new policy input. Since k>=1, both plans sharing a first sign share the same first action; the improvement, if any, comes through estimating which immediate sign to choose.
