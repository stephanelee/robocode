## Finding: rating prediction differs from the angle actually selected/fired

Core.java248–268 computes eligible gun angles using the next-own-state/future-origin virtual context, selects one and places only its angle/context-lite metadata in FireControl.Plan. On the next decision, a still-valid plan can fire at its required current heading. Core.java191–204 then constructs a fresh request-turn shotContext and RECOMPUTES analytic/long/recent predictions from the new scan and stores. Those new predictions become the confirmed outgoing common cohort at475–502 and are later scored by Learner.trainGun. The selected plan's original prediction ensemble is not retained.

Thus gun ratings can evaluate an angle different from the physical angle attributed to that same gun in the submitted plan. The common cohort remains internally common and immutable after request creation, but it measures fresh counterfactual recommendations, not necessarily the prepared/fired gun decisions. Specification8.2's frozen-prediction/non-recomputation rule and3.3's prior-plan/current-heading execution need one coherent contract here. Do not describe these ratings as evidence that the selected physical gun fired its rated prediction. Calling them purely fresh counterfactual quality would require an explicit design decision/limitation, not silent equivalence to prepared-shot accuracy.

Recommended separately specified correction: preserve a coherent immutable prediction set/selected gun/angle/context version with the firing plan, carry it through acceptance/confirmation, and rate the predictions belonging to the actual prepared opportunity; validate actual origin/heading compatibility rather than silently substituting a fresh set. Alternatively specify a fully explicit fresh-counterfactual selector contract and separate its diagnostics from physical planned-gun attribution.
