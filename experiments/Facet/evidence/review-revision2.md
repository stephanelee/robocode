# Independent Facet review: preliminary blockers

2026-11-04. Reviewer wrote no Facet gameplay code. Inputs: supplied specification; new Facet implementation only; official Tank Royale 1.4.0 source contracts and frozen runtime; independently constructed legal own-state diagnostic. No restricted opponent source, datasets, archives or source-derived expected decisions read. These are preliminary findings while implementation is still in progress, not approval to benchmark or merge.

1. **Physics.java, velocity/step — exact engine law required.** Zero target speed stops immediately before translation. Reversal from positive v toward negative target uses v/2-1, mirrored for negative v; it is not gradual subtraction2. Translation uses old body heading and newly calculated speed; body rotation limit uses new speed; independent gun/radar rates apply after translation. Wall/corner clipping constrains along the old-to-new movement segment (sequential x/y bounds), then speed becomes0. Supplied actual trace fixtures validate866 one-step transitions at <=1e-6, including both signs, finite-distance API requests, walls and corner. Do not replace exact engine behavior with familiar classic physics.

2. **Waves.Service.shadows — unsupported safety credit.** Current implementation compares broad radial distance ranges and then shadows the entire angular sweep of an own-bullet segment. A radial-shell crossing somewhere in a segment does not establish interception at every angle on that sweep at the enemy bullet's corresponding arrival time. Solve the relative-time line/radial intersection and credit only angular intervals supported by that intersection and event uncertainty. Death before possible intersection must remove future credit; same-tick death with unknown intra-turn phase must be conservative. Do not call a full segment conservative shadow: it is optimistic defense. Until validated, return no reduction and expose a shadow-fallback counter; this is a documented incomplete capability, not full specification completion.

3. **Geometry.integrate — fallback is not a proven conservative maximum.** Above81 quadrature points, sampling81 uniformly around the full circle and taking their maximum can miss arbitrarily narrow Gaussian kernels. Use a mathematically valid upper bound supplied by density construction (sum/normalized sum of kernel maxima plus prior bound), or bounded maximization with certified error; do not claim conservative density from sparse samples. Add a narrow peak positioned midway between fallback samples fixture.

4. **Learner.Context/Wave/trainGun — missing-origin quality discarded.** context() computes quality.5 for missing40-turn history, but Wave stores no origin-quality field and trainGun uses only outcome quality. Carry frozen origin-quality and missing-feature flags with each wave; combine with outcome quality exactly once and halve missing-feature observations. Never retroactively recompute origin features. Test a reliable outcome on an incomplete origin and assert reduced training weight.

5. **Learner.density — CV prior direction and normalization.** Frozen lateral feature is already signed. Multiplying it by w.sign again changes a negative-orbit target's cold constant-velocity mode into the opposite direction. Use the legal engine-native CV interception angle from the wave's frozen self state. The uniform20% prior must have theoretical escape support; applying its constant density across the entire circle produces a nonnormalized mixture. Validate mirrored orbits and integrate the actual prior across its full support to1 within declared approximation.

6. **Energy.Ledger.scan — heat creation phase.** Confirmed actual trace establishes fire request at decision55, confirmed creation56 with heat1+p/5 (1.02 for p.1), while gun rotation and movement are already visible in own state56. Do not subtract a cooling turn from the creation heat. Request/current-heading origin is state55; BulletFiredEvent56 contains origin55; own BulletState56 has already moved one bullet-speed step. Represent decision/request/creation/first-motion clocks separately. Radius anchored to origin state55 gives first visible radius speed at56; anchoring at confirmation56 requires phase+1. Add tests rejecting one-turn-early heat forecasts and radius lag. Energy-only exact shot inference must use the scan visibility phase and compatible heat interval without inventing certainty from gaps/collision possibilities.

7. **Learner.gun survivor data — displacement policy absent.** A melee-origin survivor duel must decode legal displacement labels in its origin effective-heading/sign frame and project via bounded fixed-point iteration; it must not silently use precise-GF label stores learned during melee. Keep model label types separate, test negative-speed effective-heading round trip and transition with retained per-target learning. If this remains gated/incomplete, label it outstanding and do not claim full specification.

8. **Learning and accounting thresholds require complete adapter review.** Current preliminary code cannot alone establish duplicate event handling, exactly-once wave training, accepted-versus-confirmed physical shots, event-batch energy correction ordering, lifecycle, bot-count/radar mismatch recovery, work quotas including context simulations/shadows, or bounded telemetry. Review the completed adapter and planner before considering approval. No competitive screen can run on partial fixture coverage.

Diagnostic provenance and exact clocks: see validation.json and motion-fixtures.json. Failed first startup retained in attempt-1/. Final acquisition one deliberately stopped diagnostic round,434 decisions per probe because server stop takes effect asynchronously after marker400. No strength or gameplay comparison is claimed. Missing actual-trace cases: rejected firing, bot-bot contacts, hostile missing scans/simultaneous hits, skipped-turn reconciliation. Existing isolated energy trace may complement this evidence but does not establish all those cases.

Completed first-pass adapter/planner review adds these blockers:

9. **FireControl.valid + Core fresh scans — firing starvation.** valid() requires target.latest.turn == plan.observationTurn. A normally locked fresh target updates every turn, so the prior plan prepared on turn t is invalidated on t+1 even when the scan is compatible and gun is aligned. Invalidate only a major incompatible scan change; validate the prior angle/origin/power against current legal evidence. Add a fresh-scan-every-turn engine-style fixture proving an aligned prepared plan submits/then confirms one shot, and a genuinely incompatible target movement fixture proving cancellation.

10. **Core BulletFiredEvent processing — wrong outgoing origin/phase.** makeOutgoing(own,target,t,...) uses current own state at confirmation t after movement/rotation; confirmed event's bullet position is prior decision/request state. Freeze target/context/predictions before outcome at request opportunity; construct physical wave from event bullet origin, event actual heading and pending request origin time, with explicit creation/first-motion clocks. Do not rerun models at confirmation after learning could change. FireControl.confirm currently checks only power and loose time window; validate actual event origin/heading and expected confirmation batch, and keep unmatched confirmations as physical bullets with unavailable targeting context rather than an invented target.

11. **Core inference aim labels lose same-batch and uncertain crossings.** Incoming contact events are associated before current batch scans can infer new incoming waves, so a short-range hit on the first scan showing a shot may be incorrectly unmatched permanently. Collect combat evidence first, apply all scan/energy inference, then associate unresolved same-batch contact evidence exactly once. Exact reliable incoming inference still sets originError=min(64,8*dt), which is8 even for consecutive scans; therefore resolve()'s originError<1e-6 requirement prevents all runtime visit labels, leaving flattener permanently untrained. Reliable origin uncertainty should reflect actual origin history/phase, while uncertain radial envelopes should never become exact labels.

12. **Core incoming center labels/denominators and forecast gating.** Incoming visits are currently learned at FULLY_PASSED using current own position, rather than a reliable center crossing with retained swept/history context. e.shots is incremented on resolved inferred waves regardless confidence/physical support, while hit/contact counters have different association subsets; the raw enemy hit denominator and Wilson activation can be inconsistent. Forecast creation uses heatLow only; heatLow is initialized0 before first scan despite heatHigh3, so a fresh target can create a nearly-cool forecast during known startup heat. Preserve plausible heat schedule intervals and explicitly label confidence; do not turn lower-bound cool into certain imminent firing.

13. **Core dedup identities — legitimate events collapsed.** HitBotEvent and HitWallEvent use an empty identity, so different same-turn victims/wall sides are treated as duplicates. Include relevant event payload such as victim ID, bearing and turn where no engine event identity exists; test a multi-contact tick and repeated identical callback separately. Chronology should include own command/state version and decision/request/confirmation clocks, not only string class/type and current state turn.

14. **Planner two-wave score — second wave omitted/duplicated along first path.** p.states stores the full first-wave trajectory through complete clearance, but branch starts at its earlier center crossing. Code charges all first-path overlaps for second-wave ticks up to crossing, and q simulation only starts crossing+1 without carrying existing overlapping interval/crossing history into q. Accumulate a union of second-wave intervals already crossed, then continue same wave at branch with that immutable history and charge one consistent integrated cost. If second fully passes before branch, q currently simulates at least an extra tick and may add missing-geometry fallback to a threat already resolved. Test second wave overlapping/passed before branch, not only a synthetic choice change.

15. **Planner fallback and hard bounds.** On quota exhaustion choose() calls emergency() even if a fully evaluated safe best already exists when later actions truncate; this may be okay if best exists is preserved (verify loop branch behavior). But total predictor steps excludes orbit safety checks, emergency trajectories, contextual reachability/escape simulations and radar next-step prediction. Share a deterministic per-decision work budget across all calls subject to the declared2400 total, and count exhausted/fallback work. Avoid claiming all quotas pass from planner.steps alone. Wave service removes oldest real threat when capacity full even if every stored wave is unresolved real; spec requires retaining unresolved real threats before low priority waves, and an explicit conservative overflow envelope/fallback for unavoidable all-real overflow.

16. **Diagnostics/model delivery gaps.** onGameStarted resets Core but Diagnostics is created once and closed at first GameEnded; a reused bot instance's next game writes closed files. No FACET_STATE_PATH model writer, persisted versioned schema, per-round model hash or immutable config consumption is implemented in the reviewed classes. Runtime properties file alone is not evidence that configuration is honored. Metrics omit required numeric damage/range/freshness/latency/GC fields and serialize counters/histograms as opaque strings. Failure disable state is checked for gun only; movement/radar retry learned paths despite three failures, contrary to required round disable behavior. These are acceptance gaps even if standalone compilation passes.

17. **Melee remains gated and incomplete in runtime.** Core unconditionally uses duel planner and gun, sets fire.plan=null when population>1, and never dispatches Melee module. This is an honest duel-stage scope only; full bot specification requires gated activation after duel correctness/review followed by separate melee protocol. Do not claim full implementation complete from an unused helper.

Additional review blockers identified during first-pass completion:

18. **Facet.main destination guard and API URI resolution.** The official API resolves the Java property `server.url`, while the reviewed entry point checks uppercase `SERVER_URL`. Validating an environment variable or a different property does not constrain the destination actually selected by the API; a conflicting lowercase property may silently redirect, or absence may fall back to interactive7654. Resolve one exact URI according to the intended supported precedence, validate its host/scheme/explicit port and reject7654, then pass that exact validated URI to the official Bot constructor. Add no-network tests for conflicting env/property values, omitted port/defaults, uppercase/lowercase mismatch, malformed/IPv6 URIs and accepted isolated endpoint. Do not launch the bot while guard equivalence is unproven.

19. **Virtual gun rating cohort.** Core currently creates/rates near-cool virtual waves irrespective of whether a physical firing opportunity was accepted and subsequently confirmed. Virtual movement waves may train movement labels, but selector ratings must use frozen predictions from the common confirmed-opportunity cohort. Keep eligibility/ratings separate from label generation; cancel the same invalid/death/unobserved outcome for all competing guns. Tests must demonstrate no rating increment for an unconfirmed near-cool wave and equal denominator increments for every eligible predictor on one confirmed resolved opportunity.

20. **Wilson denominator double exclusion.** Core resolve() already increments e.shots only for non-contact waves. Learner.density then evaluates `e.shots-e.contacts`, excluding bullet-bullet contacts a second time and potentially making denominator smaller than hit numerator or negative. Use explicitly named counters such as resolvedPhysicalCouldHit, actualHits and contactEvidence, define each event's exactly-once contribution, and test Wilson gate with contacts, unmatched evidence and uncertain waves. Reject nonfinite probabilities rather than allowing NaN to suppress activation silently.

21. **Analytic intercept after wall contact.** Physics.intercept clamps independently projected x/y coordinates to center bounds, which continues velocity parallel to a wall after the real bot would have stopped at first segment contact. Use the same engine-native motion/segment clipping law as the predictor, with declared unknown-command hypothesis; forecast no post-collision sliding under the constant-command startup model. Test an oblique wall impact and a corner where pointwise coordinate clamping changes intercept angle.

22. **Terminal batch drain and exactly-once round metrics.** onRoundEnded snapshots before draining events that may still be queued by the final onTick. The run loop can stop before another core.decide, losing the final fired/hit/contact/death evidence, suppressing energy/counter updates and leaving pending requests unreconciled. onGameEnded also snapshots the same round again, producing duplicate round metric rows. Separate observation reconciliation from command planning: drain each legal final event batch exactly once using the final own-state version, resolve/cancel pending wave/shot states as appropriate, then finalize one metrics row keyed by game identity plus round. Terminal drain must emit no movement, radar, gun or fire intent and must not manufacture a next decision turn. A game-ended callback following round-ended must neither repeat training nor append a second row. A reused process/new GameStarted must create cold models, fresh transient state, live bounded writers and a new game identity without retaining earlier opponent IDs as identity guarantees. Add a lifecycle fixture with final confirmed shot/hit and death callbacks in both legal terminal callback orders, duplicate terminal notifications, and two games in one process. Assert exact confirmed shot/hit counts, label emission at most once, no terminal commands, exactly one row per completed (game,round), cold second-game learners, and usable second-game telemetry. Do not benchmark until this holds.
