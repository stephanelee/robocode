# Adaptive Prime: pinned-source opponent analysis

This is a source audit of **Adaptive Prime1.0**, repository `fduch-stranger/robocode-bot` at `67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf`, including its shared `bots/bot_core`. It is not a new benchmark, a ranking, or a finding that one mechanism caused our losses. Source was read from the byte-verified native packages used in the retained experiments; the [110-file source and12-wheel lock](../../tools/opponents/strong-melee.lock.json) preserves hashes, sizes, dependency versions and licenses. Adaptive/shared code uses the repository Apache2.0 license. The bot explicitly accepts1v1 and melee. [Identity and composition](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/adaptive-prime/adaptive-prime.py#L79-L145).

## What our evidence establishes

The separate [PR51 baseline](../improvements/advanced-python-duels.md) measured AIS at **3/60 first places,17.683050% equal-game score share**,270/2,631 hits,1,464.900003 damage dealt versus3,851.8 taken, two wall contacts and zero AIS skips. That used frozen AIS946/JAR9679, before warm-up/refactor; do not silently describe it as a newly measured current version. The [strong melee baseline](../improvements/strong-melee-baseline.md) gave Adaptive4/60 first places/24.396867% share, versus Diamond51/60. Neither small roster establishes a universal strength order.

The [PR55 blend experiment](../improvements/blended-duel-danger.md) is also a useful negative control: its advanced screen improved, but fresh Adaptive confirmation fell17.639776→16.750978% (1/40 first places in each arm). This does not identify the responsible mechanism. Existing aggregates contain neither Adaptive's chosen-gun history nor matched-profile activation counts; damage totals are not attribution.

The upstream [README](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/adaptive-prime/README.md#L1-L66) calls this a champion *candidate* and reports local BasicGFSurfer experiments. Those are author reports, not independently verified league titles or our confirmation data. Diamond Port is a different bot; do not substitute its gun implementation for this one.

## Gun: several distinct learned hypotheses

The actual runtime factory constructs head-on, linear, displacement, traditional GF and dynamic-cluster guns. **Default live selection allows linear, traditional GF, dynamic cluster and displacement; head-on exists but is not ordinarily selectable. There is no circular gun in that factory.** Default mode is linear. Our packaging clears `ROBOCODE_*` overrides rather than forcing an easier gun. [Factory](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/gun/factory.py#L16-L62), [live modes and shared policy](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/gun/policy.py#L12-L37).

- **Dynamic-cluster GF:** the effective Adaptive threshold is **30 observed samples**, not the standalone component's60 default: policy conversion overrides it. It takes17 nearest neighbors from a buffer capped at1,200 total/900 per target. Seven features are arena-normalized range, power, absolute lateral speed, signed advancing speed, acceleration, time since velocity change and wall clearance. Additional fire context records lateral direction/confidence, flight time, wall-limited escape balance and movement tags derived from recent observations. Thus “change speed because its gun assumes constant velocity” is an inaccurate diagnosis. [Effective configuration conversion](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/gun/policy.py#L332-L372), [features/context](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/gun/context.py#L223-L292), [neighbor weighting](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/gun/guns/dynamic_cluster/gun.py#L121-L168).
- Neighbors are weighted by feature distance and fire-context agreement; the default recency half-life is0, meaning **no recency decay** until bounded-buffer eviction. Prediction evaluates31 GF density locations, uses a local peak centroid, and shrinks the aim by0.8 when a second peak is at least85% as strong. It does not simply average all future positions like our rejected ridge forecaster. [Defaults](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/gun/guns/dynamic_cluster/config.py#L8-L41), [peak selection](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/gun/guns/dynamic_cluster/gun.py#L170-L201).
- **Traditional GF:**31 bins, smoothed/decayed observations, with global and segmented profiles. The segment combines flight-time bands below20/35 turns, lateral speed below2/5.6 and normalized wall distance below0.12/0.25. Sparse segments blend toward global data; the GF aim is bounded to±0.87. Its availability and switching evidence differ from KNN's. [Profile and segmentation](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/gun/guns/traditional_gf/gun.py#L22-L60), [global/segment blending](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/gun/guns/traditional_gf/gun.py#L108-L185).
- **Displacement:** up to80 recent observed target positions support ranked historical-path replays at the current bullet speed. It requires enough history, ranks candidate starting contexts, and combines replay bearings around a dense angular region. A repeated orbit or deterministic stop cycle can feed this model even if linear aiming fails. [History and interpolation](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/gun/context.py#L49-L164), [historical replay](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/gun/guns/displacement/gun.py#L81-L201).

Learning labels are **legal observed wave passages**, not private enemy truth or only actual bullet hits. A requested shot creates a pending cohort; `on_bullet_fired` confirms it. When its wave reaches a scanned target, the code interpolates between observed positions, derives GF and scores every frozen virtual bearing. This interpolation is an estimator, not actual engine collision truth. Default optional evaluation-only waves are disabled. Virtual score falls linearly with angular error across an18px footprint; the aggregate combines70% exponential score (alpha0.12) and30% nonzero-score frequency, with global/segment blending. This is an empirical selection score, not a calibrated physical hit probability. [Pending/confirmed lifecycle](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/adaptive-prime/adaptive-prime.py#L1290-L1352), [passage labels](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/gun/system.py#L365-L433), [scoring](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/gun/scoring.py#L20-L124).

Selection uses visit/score thresholds, switching margins, context/role bonuses and low-support penalties. Adaptive's base switch margin is0.08; the KNN base minimum is12 visits/0.03 score, but it still cannot provide a prediction before its30 samples exist. Traditional GF and displacement have separate thresholds. The gun can adapt within a game; exploiting an early linear phase does not imply later rounds remain easy. [Adaptive policy](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/adaptive-prime/adaptive_config.py#L107-L140), [selection machinery](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/gun/aim.py#L41-L226).

## Defense: more than two analytic aim rays

Energy-drop detection subtracts known bullet/ram/wall corrections and rejects stale gaps above4 or corrected drops outside0.1–3.0. It discounts small close-collision drops. Bullet damage/bonus corrections are delayed until the next scan; wall corrections use the same scan. A separate seven-neighbor model predicts firepower from own/enemy energy and range, with gun-heat estimates producing optional expected waves. These are inferred threats, not visible enemy bullets. [Detection](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/energy/fire_detection.py#L37-L100), [drop filters](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/energy/drops.py#L8-L78), [correction visibility](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/energy/corrections.py#L1-L62), [power predictor](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/energy/fire_power.py#L25-L84).

Its movement profile learns **every inferred wave passage with weight1**, and matching actual hits with extra weight3. It is partly a visitation-flattening distribution, not a pure hit histogram. Base profiles are target/range/GF bins; twelve additional buffers split range, lateral/advancing velocity, acceleration, wall clearance, flight-time and deceleration-age combinations. The confidence-weighted ensemble can only raise base danger, and an unvisited-bin floor0.08 remains. [Wave passage/hit updates](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/movement/flattener.py#L163-L231), [buffer dimensions](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/movement/profile.py#L80-L166), [danger combination](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/movement/danger.py#L14-L35).

Default1v1 movement first considers **clockwise, stop, counter-clockwise**, predicting up to80 ticks against two selected waves. It integrates danger across the body's GF footprint while the bullet ring crosses it, scales by damage and time-to-impact, adds a head-on prior, then the cheapest continuation for the next wave. Current-option danger receives5% hysteresis. This explicitly includes stopping and multiwave continuation; “it only orbits” is false. Wave choice sorts radial remaining distance, not a complete search over every live wave. Confirmed own bullets can lower predicted danger through geometric shadow bins (multiplier0.45), an estimate rather than guaranteed interception. [Option and wave selection](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/movement/flattener.py#L360-L424), [lookahead/weighting](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/movement/option_surfing.py#L203-L367), [default limits](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/movement/config.py#L5-L65).

When no option wave is usable, it tries go-to surfing, then a potential field combining enemy/wall repulsion, orbiting, centering and fire-threat response. Duel preference is480px, minimum380, critical300, conservative wall margin130; finite-distance movement uses forward/back-as-front navigation. Physics prediction updates speed, moves along old body heading, then turns, with line clipping and speed-zero at its virtual boundary. This is source intent, not an independently exhaustive conformance proof. Melee instead prioritizes minimum-risk destinations with commitment/hysteresis when multiple targets are known. [Movement hierarchy](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/adaptive-prime/adaptive-prime.py#L517-L685), [duel policy](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/adaptive-prime/adaptive_config.py#L328-L353), [motion predictor](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/physics/movement.py#L23-L108).

## Radar, shooting economy, resets and runtime

Radar predicts1–3 ticks of target motion, uses range-scaled overscan2–8 degrees and a24-degree turn cap; stale scans trigger reacquisition/search. A target older than1 turn fails the normal firing gate; age above4 changes tracking behavior and above9 drops the target. Target choice includes current-target/recent-threat, range, energy and age terms, with different melee weights. [Radar implementation](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/radar.py#L65-L122), [track/fire path](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/adaptive-prime/adaptive-prime.py#L337-L448), [target scoring](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/adaptive-prime/adaptive-prime.py#L1069-L1112).

Power is not fixed. At ordinary energy it uses2.2 below160px when own energy exceeds36 (otherwise1.6),1.8 below280, then1.3/1.6 or1.0/1.3 depending on evidence/energy lead, and0.8 beyond620. At energy≤18 it falls to0.8 close/0.6 far. A close finisher estimates target energy/3.5+0.2, bounded0.6–2.2. Energy≤7 enables a last-stand path capped0.6 with0.1 reserve. Normal fire gate uses7-degree alignment, one-turn memory and5energy margin; last stand tightens alignment to3 degrees/range320. This is not an exact range-scaled ray-footprint gate or strict minimum-damage optimizer. Optional dynamic-quality power scaling is **disabled by default**. [Power branches](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/adaptive-prime/adaptive-prime.py#L861-L940), [defaults](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/adaptive-prime/adaptive_config.py#L143-L229), [fire-gate branches](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/energy/fire_gate.py#L41-L103).

A new round clears targets, live gun/movement waves, target history, heat and timing-local decisions, but retains learned gun scores/KNN/GF profiles and movement statistics within the game. New-game handling clears opponent learning. KNN and traditional-GF round/target-removal methods deliberately do nothing to learned memory. Cold games therefore differ from successive rounds of one game. [Bot resets](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/adaptive-prime/adaptive-prime.py#L996-L1046), [gun state boundaries](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/gun/system.py#L300-L324), [KNN retained memory](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/gun/guns/dynamic_cluster/gun.py#L455-L459).

Our runtime was CPython3.13 with native BotAPI1.3.1 against the1.4.0 runner; the exact12-wheel environment is locked. The code avoids full multi-gun aim while heat exceeds three cooling turns, tracking a cached offset up to20 turns old before full aim resumes. This reduces work, but is not measured evidence of deadline safety or an exploitable pause. Debug/telemetry output is environment-controlled and was not secretly enabled/tuned in our frozen opponent. We have no captured per-turn Adaptive CPU/GC/skip trace; AIS zero-skip metrics and Retcon native timing cannot establish Adaptive timing. [Hot tracking](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/adaptive-prime/adaptive-prime.py#L450-L477), [debug enablement](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/debug.py#L92-L110).

## Testable counters and their costs

These are hypotheses for synthesis, **not promoted fixes or measured wins**. No new source or games are authorized by this document alone.

| Source-grounded limitation | Candidate response / mechanism to verify | Counter-risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New movement waves are rejected outside150–700px; expected heat waves require at least220px. Existing older waves may remain usable after closing. [Wave eligibility](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/bot_core/movement/flattener.py#L93-L112), [heat-wave eligibility](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/adaptive-prime/adaptive-prime.py#L302-L335) | Controlled close pressure could shorten bullet flight and reduce *new* surfing-wave coverage; measure actual achieved range, opponent option changes, damage and wins. | It actively opens range, has strong close firepower and may keep older waves. Closing can increase incoming hits/ramming or trap our bot at walls. The150px boundary is not a guaranteed blind spot. |
| Finite context/memory with no default KNN recency decay; recent-path replay favors recurring motion. | Change movement phases from seeded legal state rather than one repetitive orbit or purely shot-clock reversal; check whether virtual accuracy actually falls over whole games. | Acceleration/time-since-change, movement tags and segmented GF already model many changes. Randomness can reduce our own lateral clearance; repeated phase schedules can also be learned. |
| Option search has three controls and two selected waves, with radial-gap ordering and estimated bullet shadows. | A coherent gun/movement strategy may exploit predicted stops or overlapping wave timing; first require a physically reachable fixture rather than an artificial ordering example. | Ammo/power variation changes damage, cooldown and learning opportunities. Exact-ring option surfing already handles stops and two-wave continuations; assuming it cannot dodge multiple shots is false. |
| Virtual angular scores and learned danger are heuristic proxies, not actual damage objective/collision truth. | Use physically consistent own fire rays, confirmed shots and short-flight predictions, then judge official score/wins. | Better virtual accuracy alone may fail in live combat; opponents adapt across rounds. Do not optimize against private opponent telemetry as an input. |

The practical first build remains a **separate Diamond-focused challenger**, using the combined Diamond/Retcon analyses to choose one coherent policy. Adaptive is a later transfer opponent unless a separate protocol names it. Production AIS must remain unchanged; successful challenger ideas require their own fresh AIS transfer comparison and regression guards. This audit narrows hypotheses without claiming that complexity, pressure, randomness or a particular AI model guarantees wins.
