# Diamond Port: pinned 1v1 opponent analysis

This analysis is for **Diamond Port1.8.28**, the native Python Tank Royale adaptation in `fduch-stranger/robocode-bot@67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf`. It is not a claim about the original Java Diamond's tournament rating. The exact110-file selected source/shared-core manifest and12-wheel Python3.13/API1.3.1 runtime are recorded in [strong-melee.lock.json](../../tools/opponents/strong-melee.lock.json). Diamond's altered-port zlib notice and shared Apache-2.0 license must stay with any distributed source. The analysis reads these pinned source bytes; it adds no opponent behavior changes or games.

## What our games establish

[PR51's separately registered baseline](../improvements/advanced-python-duels.md) measured the old accepted946/JAR9679 default bot against this pin: **0/60 wins,3.885963% equal-game score share**, ranks2/2/2. AIS hit72 of1160 shots (6.206897%), dealt383.6 damage and took5050.5;9 walls,0 bot collisions,0 AIS skips. All three cold20-round models and losses are retained. This shows a severe whole-bot gap in that regime, not which component caused it. Diamond's private per-turn timing was not recorded; AIS's zero-skips is not evidence of Diamond's zero-skips.

Later replacement/blend experiments are separate evidence, including their rejection decisions; they do not turn this baseline into a comparison with a renamed candidate. In particular, increased offline danger plausibility or a small development gain is not a demonstrated way to beat Diamond. No historical result is pooled into a fresh challenger confirmation.

## Architecture and adaptation lifetime

The entry point executes movement, gun and radar, then calls `go()`. Its adapter converts Tank Royale's east-zero/counterclockwise headings to classic Robocode radians, supplies only scans/own state/events, and implements back-as-front movement. Components are created once per battle; round initialization clears trajectories/round state while retaining enemy learning views and virtual-gun ratings. `GameStartedEvent` discards those components, so a fresh process/game is a cold population. This is why a20-round game tests adaptation, not20 independent cold duels. [Entry/adapter](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond-port.py#L253), [round/game resets](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond-port.py#L319), [enemy reset keeps views](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/enemy.py#L85), [virtual-gun round reset](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/gun.py#L950).

The experimental counterbot must not read Diamond's views, wave queues, selected gun, RNG, native telemetry or private timings. Source-informed strategy is allowed; runtime decisions must use our own legal scans, state, commands and confirmed events.

## Gun: strengths and actual conditioning

**Cold start is already trained.** In an initial1v1, Diamond starts with a fixed evolved1000-point PerceptualGun, not a naive head-on gun. After at least9 recorded duel wave breaks, it drops that startup gun and chooses between TripHammerKnnGun and AntiSurferGun using virtual ratings. The counter is **wave breaks, not9 actual hits or even9 fired shots**. Both learning views enable virtual-wave visits, so withholding our own fire or assuming a long untrained opening does not prevent its gun learning our movement. [Gun initialization/switch](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/gun.py#L982), [1000-point startup](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/gun.py#L747), [counter definition](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/gun.py#L523), [visit labels](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/gun.py#L209).

**It conditions on the behavior AIS has been repeating.** The1v1 TripHammer vector includes bullet power, flight-time/range, signed velocity, relative heading sine/cosine, acceleration, forward/reverse wall availability, time since velocity change normalized by flight time, and virtuality. Feature weights evolve with accumulated view size. Its main view uses up to225 nearest neighbors, with `k` growing approximately as data size/9, and evaluates59 candidate firing angles with a compact density kernel. AntiSurfer uses related movement/wall/change-time features, four recency windows capped at125/400/1500/4000 observations, and small neighbor sets. Its1v1 labels are direction-normalized, wall-aware precise guess factors, not one global raw bin histogram. [TripHammer features](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/gun.py#L109), [KNN aim/weight schedule](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/gun.py#L682), [AntiSurfer](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/gun.py#L613), [precise signed GF](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/wave.py#L378).

**Selection rewards useful near-misses too.** VirtualGunsManager logs a prediction from each gun at a firing wave, then adds a smooth angular-error score normalized by target angular width and escape range. Its rating is not raw actual bullet-hit percentage. Round changes clear unresolved virtual bullets, not accumulated ratings. Therefore simply switching AIS from head-on/linear danger to sparse hit bins does not remove Diamond's ability to learn the resulting control policy. [Virtual scoring](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/gun.py#L893).

**Aiming and firing are staged.** It attempts the previously aimed shot before computing the next aim. Expensive learned aiming is deferred until within3 cooling ticks; otherwise it tracks a direct bearing and updates gun selection. Firing requires exactly zero gun heat and zero gun-turn remaining, plus energy strictly greater than power. These are source facts, not proof of a timing bug or a guaranteed one-tick exploit. A countergun must itself respect the engine's current-heading/next-shot contract. [Aim/fire sequence](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/gun.py#L1038), [fire gate](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/gun.py#L1114).

## Movement: why a generic gun struggles

**Two-wave option surfing.** Diamond chooses clockwise, counterclockwise or stop, predicts an escape destination, integrates danger over the swept bot footprint as the wave crosses it, and recurses over the next wave when the cutoff permits. It includes bullet damage, inverse time-to-impact, distance pressure and outgoing-bullet shadow factors. This is not avoidance of two fixed head-on/linear aim points. It refreshes neighbor caches when the surfed wave or hit evidence changes. Its no-surf-wave orbit and surfing distance controller both prefer **650px**, with160px wall smoothing. [Distance/orbit/options](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/move.py#L742), [recursive danger and time/shadow weights](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/move.py#L860).

**Several data regimes, not one sparse profile.** Surf views include a simple hit view, normal and strongly weighted recent-hit views, plus light/full visit-based flatteners enabled at normalized hit-rate/error-margin thresholds. Recent windows and exponential decay adapt when our gun changes. If no view is enabled, default danger emphasizes GF0 with weight3 and GF0.85 with weight1. This cold prior is a possible first-design consideration, not evidence that an untested fixed offset will beat the bot; its views respond to hits. [View definitions](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/move.py#L189), [threshold mechanics](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/knn_view.py#L99), [danger/default prior](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/move.py#L975).

**It anticipates fire before observing an energy drop.** Using inferred enemy gun heat, guessed power and legal scan/motion history, it adds imaginary waves when a surf slot is empty. Actual energy-drop detection requires plausible power and an inferred cool gun, with wall-damage correction; own bullet damage and hit bonuses are queued so a scan sees the appropriate pre-impact energy before the correction. Unknown shot timing and power remain estimates. This weakens the simplistic idea that only changing firing cadence makes its defense disappear. [Imaginary waves](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/move.py#L385), [detection and forecast](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/move.py#L645), [deferred energy changes](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond-port.py#L332).

## Radar, power and positioning constraints

Radar locks the only known opponent, overshoots its current bearing by22.5°, otherwise sweeps at45° toward unseen/stalest locations. It uses a predicted own location and resets scan integrity after25 turns of a participant-count mismatch. Radar is independent of body and gun. We have no measured radar-loss weakness; a close pass can raise angular demand but Diamond explicitly reacquires. [Radar implementation](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/radar.py#L24), [decoupling](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond-port.py#L282).

Normal1v1 power is1.95; distance below150px **or a rammer classification** raises it to2.95. Beyond325px it can reduce power cubically when its energy is below an opponent-dependent35–63 threshold. It caps at enemy energy/4, applies a minimum0.1 and its available energy, and fires only with energy greater than that power. Thus pressure may shorten our bullet flight but also triggers stronger return fire; low-energy opponents are not simply abandoned, and an energy-zero target still gets a minimum shot. [Power policy](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/gun.py#L1069), [rammer evidence](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/gun.py#L383).

## Concrete port-fidelity limits

These are source differences, not measured explanations of our losses or guaranteed exploits:

1. **Prediction order differs.** `MovementPredictor.predict` turns, computes velocity, then moves on the new heading. Tank Royale1.4 uses updated speed, movement on the old heading, then bounded rotation. The port explicitly retains classic ordering for its coupled danger/escape model. This affects self-projection, escape angles and aiming geometry. [Predictor](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/movement_predictor.py#L1).
2. **Stopping and reversal predictions differ too.** With velocity8, long positive distance and max velocity0, its `_get_new_velocity` returns6; pinned Tank Royale target speed0 returns0 immediately. For a reversal from+8 with negative long distance it predicts+6, while the pinned Tank Royale rule returns+3. The port's actual adapter commands still go through the official API/engine; it is the forecast that differs. Our counter must predict the real engine, not copy these classic assumptions. [Classic velocity calculation](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/movement_predictor.py#L103), [pinned Tank Royale speed law](https://github.com/robocode-dev/tank-royale/blob/c8ad3a8d19a843f6258d6f6f9db7f29229963903/server/src/main/kotlin/dev/robocode/tankroyale/server/rules/math.kt#L24).
3. **Footprints differ.** Its wave intersection integrates the classic36×36 square's corners/sides, while Tank Royale's bullet collision uses the18px-radius bot circle. Its arena helper is low-edge inclusive/high-edge exclusive and keeps the18px half-width. Conservative or differently shaped danger estimates can change narrow decisions; they do not make its overall defense weak. [Wave footprint](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/wave.py#L455), [field/wall smoothing](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/dia_utils.py#L277).
4. **Requested fire is treated as immediate internally.** The adapter records a pending accepted fire, and the gun notifies bullet-shadow listeners at request time/location; `on_bullet_fired` clears that pending flag rather than reconstructing every internal wave from the confirmed event. Tank Royale reports real bullet creation through its event. The exact error for a decision needs an event trace; the source alone does not prove an exploitable universal one-tick offset. [Adapter acceptance](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond-port.py#L243), [listener](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/gun.py#L1174), [confirmation handler](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond-port.py#L412).

The port catches decision/event exceptions and continues; missing precise intersections can be skipped instead of throwing. It freezes Python GC survivors at round initialization and has optional stage timing. These are robustness/runtime facts, not authorization to target resource exhaustion or manufacture opponent skips. We have not measured its latency distribution in our duels. [Loop/error handling](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond-port.py#L292), [intersection fallback](https://github.com/fduch-stranger/robocode-bot/blob/67b68c22e41c18281a3fce9ef638e3c23a0b47cf/bots/ports/diamond-port/diamond_port/wave.py#L455).

## Counterplans compared prospectively

| Design | Why it addresses a real mechanism | Main counter/tradeoff | Current disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Short-range interception pressure plus independently timed flank changes; coordinated close-range aiming/energy | Tries to deny650px distancing and reduce the time available for two-wave surfing. Irregular future phase durations add uncertainty beyond a deterministic shot-triggered path. | Closing exposes us to the learned gun, retreating target and2.95 close/rammer power. Reversals can lower lateral speed and make head-on hits easier. Pressure may never achieve the intended range. | First **experimental DiamondChallenger hypothesis**, not a predicted winner. Must measure achieved range, damage/shot, survival, collisions and actual wins. |
| Context-conditioned online displacement/GF gun plus robust learned defense | A gun trained on fresh scan outcomes and our own confirmed-shot context can learn which movement options Diamond actually takes; segmented danger can model more than head-on/linear. | Requires sufficient representative legal data, accurate timing/footprints and nonstationary safeguards. Diamond's AntiSurfer/recency views adapt too. A global sparse hit histogram has already proved inadequate as an unconditional replacement. | Alternative if the bounded pressure prototype fails; no new NN/data framework or automatic family search. |
| Explicit opponent option forecasts using the real engine | STOP/CW/CCW are concrete source choices; timing an aim around observed stops or constrained walls could improve prediction. | Its chosen option depends on private learned danger, wave shadows and history we do not observe. We may forecast a family of possibilities but cannot import its private state or assert exact prediction. | Useful later gun hypothesis, not permission to replay hidden opponent internals. |

Simple fixed-offset targeting, total gun silence, always ramming or assuming radar failure are not established counters. We will not claim success merely because the new bot is separate or survives longer.

## Experimental implementation and validation boundary

DiamondChallenger is to be a distinct standalone official-API bot, descriptor, entry point and JAR with private `CHALLENGER_*` seed/model/metrics paths. It must not subclass/rename AIS, use production checkpoints, change AIS source/JAR, or read opponent artifacts. A separate small Gradle project may reuse unchanged pure geometry/radar/physics helpers; production build inputs remain identical. Its gun state snapshots are its own cold-game format, not DQN checkpoints or exact process-resumption claims.

The first coherent prototype may combine pressure, radar, legal virtual targeting and energy because it is explicitly a whole-bot design; no isolated causal credit follows from its score. Freeze its exact source/JAR, motion constants, source/runtime/opponent pins, budget, labels and decision rules before matches; get independent exact premeasurement clearance. Preserve every loss, invalid acquisition and model endpoint. A development win is not repeatable superiority: any promotion to a Diamond-beating claim needs genuinely fresh fixed confirmation. Only then consider transferring an attributable idea into AIS with Retcon and Adaptive guards. Melee remains parked.
