# Retcon 0.2.0 1v1 baseline — 2 November 2026

AISurvivor struggles against this stronger Java opponent. The fixed minimum-risk policy won one of five evaluation rounds; cold and trained DQN policies won none. After 20 training rounds, the trained policy's score and hit rate were lower than the cold policy in this short, unpaired experiment. This is a useful challenge baseline, not a statistically established ranking of policies.

## Measured results

| Phase/policy | Rounds | AISurvivor wins | AISurvivor score | Retcon score | AISurvivor hit rate | Wall collisions | Skipped turns |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Fixed minimum risk | 5 | 1 | 248 | 592 | 13.0% | 4 | 0 |
| Cold DQN | 5 | 0 | 173 | 844 | 18.2% | 23 | 0 |
| DQN training | 20 | 0 | 554 | 3279 | 17.7% | 194 | 0 |
| Trained DQN evaluation | 5 | 0 | 79 | 785 | 11.4% | 24 | 0 |

All four actual matches completed: 20 training + 15 evaluation = 35 rounds. Training uses a different budget and is not directly comparable with five-round evaluation totals. Evaluation score share was 29.5% fixed, 17.0% cold, and 9.1% trained. With one seed and five evaluation rounds, these differences are diagnostic observations rather than a reliable estimate of long-run policy superiority.

Cold DQN dealt 121.63 damage while taking 393.85; trained evaluation dealt 74.33 while taking 403.73. Fixed movement dealt 187.73 while taking 313.24. Wall collisions averaged 0.8 per round for fixed movement, 4.6 for cold DQN, and 4.8 for trained DQN. No AISurvivor skipped turns were recorded. These observations motivate inspecting movement and wall handling before adding model complexity.

Strict telemetry checks verified the requested modes, seed, unique 1-based round IDs, checkpoint status, training counters, finite metrics, and unchanged evaluation checkpoint hashes. This was a real server 1.4.0 match with Retcon's 1.3.1 API, establishing compatibility beyond the startup smoke marker. No baseline attempt failed in this run. The final PR restores one-round Retcon smoke on normal PRs/pushes; full 35-round experiments remain manually selectable.

## Limits and next work

The first follow-up is a controlled candidate-versus-previous-version comparison, using the hypotheses in [the three-cycle log](../improvements.md). Repeat larger evaluations before accepting a strength claim. Retain failed hypotheses and regressions in that log. The old sample baseline remains unchanged for historical comparison, and sample smoke remains a regression check. The first challenge focuses on 1v1; sample melee remains available as a regression suite.

The dashboard is regenerated from canonical JSON and covered by automated/static report checks. Browser visual inspection was not attempted through an alternate surface after the earlier local-URL policy block.
