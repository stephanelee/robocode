# Cooldown retargeting opportunity test

Prospective offline opportunity test, before scored computation. Parent source and reader 9c392efc488295245859a306eeecc98e1d166591 (#140); measured Crossfire JAR and original 12 candidate recordings / 60 rounds from #138. A separate reviewer must clear the exact implementation commit and deterministic fixtures before the scored pass. Publish that SHA here before execution.

On each live melee decision reconstruct incumbent selection and circular intercept with the measured JAR, own scans through the current tick, public deaths and own observer pose. Use the same JAR power, width and arena-constrained intercept. Both arms get the same incumbent and observed movement/radar. Baseline uses the actual Crossfire fire selector. Shadow gun state starts from the first own melee tick each round. Advance its ray by the commanded error clamped to ±20° per turn with body compensation. On a cold decision fire along the current pre-rotation ray for creation on t+1. Heat on a shot is exactly 1+p/5 with no immediate cooling; otherwise subtract setup cooling rate down to zero, without epsilon. Use the pinned server bytecode and fixtures to verify. No cross-round or duel-mode state transfer. Exclude decisions missing a verifiable next observer tick and report them. Include last-melee shots whose next tick is a duel.

Fixed policy: while hot, count remaining cooling ticks by repeated subtraction. On the current self pose and latest scans take each target’s current circular intercept as a fixed bearing for the reachability test. Protect the incumbent if its absolute aim error is within 20° × cooling ticks plus its ordinary 0.9 angular half-width AND its current scan age plus cooling ticks is ≤12 AND affordable under the ordinary reserve. Otherwise consider living nonincumbents scanned 0–3 turns ago, range strictly <500, affordable and their bearing within the same turn budget plus their own tolerance. Choose shortest range then lowest ID. Commit that target until the first cold decision; abandon on death, change to incumbent, age >12, range ≥500, or failed reserve. Recompute circular aim each tick. After cancellation hot decisions may select again. On cold decisions retain ordinary Crossfire fire selection (incumbent priority, alternate age ≤3, current-ray alignment); release commitment and resume incumbent tracking immediately. No special firing exemptions. All policy decisions use only current legal observations.

Account for counterfactual energy as observed energy plus cumulative baseline shot costs minus cumulative shadow shot costs from earlier decisions, capped above at observed energy (no spending saved energy above observed, no additional hit reward). This freezes environmental damage/hit benefits on the real trajectory and does not forecast counterfactual collisions, hits, movement, death or duel outcomes. Shadow requests consume full power and subsequent heat. For nonpositive decision energy suppress firing and model disabled gun turn as zero.

Match shots by (recording, round, decision turn) with observable t+1; count shared same target, shared redirected, shadow-only created, baseline-only displaced, neither, commitments and rejects, with per-game and intended-target breakdowns. Targets describe intent. A shifted shot counts once on each side. Require the calibrated original selector to match all 4,042 observed physical shots by owner/turn/power/ray with no missing or extra, and self-propagated unmodified gun physics to agree with the original before interpreting the shadow. Strict gate: aggregate created > displaced. No alternate candidate, threshold tuning, score inference or live game during this stage. Failure ends this route. Only a pass permits a distinct candidate and separately frozen, independently cleared live comparison (Diamond duels and top-four melee, Retcon/sample guards). Original evidence, bots, #139 and its spent receipt stay intact.
