# Combat contract: Tank Royale 1.4.0

The [classic Robocode introduction](https://robocode.sourceforge.io/) distinguishes the Java arena from the networked Tank Royale platform. Use the official Tank Royale [coordinates](https://robocode.dev/articles/coordinates-and-angles.html), [physics](https://robocode.dev/articles/physics.html) and [collision guide](https://robocode.dev/articles/collision-mechanics.html) for orientation. The repository pins the Java API and runner to **1.4.0**. Where a prose rule omits a boundary, the implementation below is the basis of the forecast.

| Question | Pinned behavior and consequence |
|---|---|
| Where is the opponent? | Origin bottom left, east 0°, north 90°, positive angles counterclockwise; classic uses north zero clockwise. The [scan schema](https://github.com/robocode-dev/tank-royale/blob/c8ad3a8d19a843f6258d6f6f9db7f29229963903/schema/schemas/scanned-bot-event.schema.yaml) sends IDs, x/y, body direction, signed speed and energy. It does not disclose gun/radar direction, bullets in flight, intended turns or policy state. |
| What happens to speed? | [math.kt](https://github.com/robocode-dev/tank-royale/blob/c8ad3a8d19a843f6258d6f6f9db7f29229963903/server/src/main/kotlin/dev/robocode/tankroyale/server/rules/math.kt#L24-L51): signed speed bounded by 8, acceleration 1 and same-sign braking up to 2. Target zero immediately yields zero; opposite target from positive v yields v/2−1 with sign symmetry for reverse. +8 to −8 starts +3, not +6. |
| Which directions apply? | [TurnProcessor](https://github.com/robocode-dev/tank-royale/blob/c8ad3a8d19a843f6258d6f6f9db7f29229963903/server/src/main/kotlin/dev/robocode/tankroyale/server/core/TurnProcessor.kt): compute new speed, translate along old heading, rotate using new-speed cap 10−0.75·abs(v). Relative gun/radar rates bounded by 20/45; Meridian explicitly decouples gun/body, radar/gun and radar/body. |
| When does a shot count? | [GunEngine](https://github.com/robocode-dev/tank-royale/blob/c8ad3a8d19a843f6258d6f6f9db7f29229963903/server/src/main/kotlin/dev/robocode/tankroyale/server/core/GunEngine.kt) checks cold gun and energy strictly greater than requested power before translation and rotation. Fire assist disabled: use current gun direction and shooter center. Initial heat 3, shot heat 1+p/5, subsequent cooling on a separate branch. Shot costs p, legal p 0.1–3, speed 20−3p. |
| Where can a bullet hit? | After motion, contacts and scans, bullets advance; wall removal precedes hit resolution. [BulletLine](https://github.com/robocode-dev/tank-royale/blob/c8ad3a8d19a843f6258d6f6f9db7f29229963903/server/src/main/kotlin/dev/robocode/tankroyale/server/model/BulletLine.kt) spans the updated position through next position: first sweep [v,2v]. Bullet/bullet uses line intersections, bullet/bot a segment and post-motion circle of radius 18. Damage 4p+2·max(0,p−1); shooter gains 3p. Observer damage can exceed remaining energy; official damage score is capped. Scan energy precedes same-tick bullet damage/bonus, so apply these corrections to the next scan interval. |
| What interrupts movement? | [CollisionDetector](https://github.com/robocode-dev/tank-royale/blob/c8ad3a8d19a843f6258d6f6f9db7f29229963903/server/src/main/kotlin/dev/robocode/tankroyale/server/core/CollisionDetector.kt) interpolates a wall contact at center margin 18; speed zero, first contact damage max(0,abs(v)/2−1). Bot overlap normally restores prior centers; rammers stop, both take 0.6. Initial overlap has special placement. If inactivity counter exceeds configured limit each bot loses 0.1 per turn; a bullet/bot hit resets counter. Zero energy disables; death requires negative energy. |
| What survives a round? | New arena state, 100 starting energy for a normal bot, heat 3, new first turn. The Java process can live across rounds; new game gets a fresh process in this experiment. The [API handler](https://github.com/robocode-dev/tank-royale/blob/c8ad3a8d19a843f6258d6f6f9db7f29229963903/bot-api/java/src/main/java/dev/robocode/tankroyale/botapi/internal/WebSocketHandler.java#L168-L196) invokes public round-end before the final queued-event flush. Official observer endpoints, not bot win callbacks, determine wins. |

[Fixtures](../../experiments/Meridian/test/FlightTest.java) exercise discrete reversal, braking, stopping, old-heading movement/new-speed turn cap, axial and oblique wall contact, current-ray tolerance, heat/energy boundaries and first sweep reach. They test the Java forecast; they do not measure battle strength or establish that enemy rays or future movement are predictable.
