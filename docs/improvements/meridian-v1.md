# Meridian v1 prospective duel protocol

Registered in [issue 76](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/issues/76) before implementation. New standalone bot; never load another bot's weights or metrics. Retcon's virtual gun selection and Diamond Port's wave gun suggest two competing risks: steady orbits are easy to extrapolate, while indiscriminate reversals invite head-on hits. Choose between forward/backward orbit trajectories by testing their clearance from plausible incoming rays; aim by comparing recent straight, turning and stopped enemy motion forecasts against subsequent scans.

One five-round game on each hard target, even on a valid loss. Require both >35% official score share, at least one official first place and zero own skips to advance. Then three fresh twenty-round games per opponent. For each require unweighted mean share >50%, >30/60 first places, at least two shares >50% and zero own skips. No reruns; an invalid attempt blocks promotion. Publish pins, IDs, seeds, private paths, metrics and independent review before the games.

**Before play amendment:** the user requires beating current AISurvivor too. [Issue update](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/issues/76) precedes all games. Run all three independent screens; all three must pass the original per-opponent gates. Maximum 15 screen + conditional 180 confirmation rounds. Cold default TRAIN AISurvivor 1.2.1 from protected master 4055578. No learning crosses game boundaries.

[Manifest](../../benchmarks/meridian-v1/games.json): IDs, seeds, order, round counts and fixed loopback ports 31410–31421. Seeds control Meridian's initial orbit choice and AIS's RNG, not engine placements or either third-party opponent's internals. Per ID, fresh build/meridian-staging/ID hosts the bot bundles and fresh build/meridian/ID holds markers.txt, full official .battle.gz, runner.log, attempt.json with endpoint/command, and for AIS only ais-model.dat and ais.jsonl. Meridian has in-memory gun scores only. No staged model exists before play. Isolated server per game; leave 7654 untouched.

Valid = exact pinned profile and identities, completed official game and all round endpoints/ticks, complete own lifecycle markers, and for AIS a cold registered seed/TRAIN metrics sequence plus final model with expected rounds. Log any invalid attempt, continue the stage's other predefined games, never replace a game. Zero own skips is a promotion/winning gate. No trial-level p-value. Report game-level shares/range and wins, confirmed fired/hit event ratio, uncapped bullet damage dealt/taken, shot power expenditure, own wall/bot contacts, and private skipped-turn callbacks. One game provides no between-game uncertainty estimate; rounds share learning. Any later candidate requires new registration, IDs and evidence.

Differencing cumulative points gives per-round shares (%) of 100.0, 0.0, 1.1, 85.5, 93.2 against Retcon; 5.2, 5.4, 5.5, 0.0, 11.1 against Diamond Port; 82.7, 74.4, 0.0, 83.5, 0.0 against AISurvivor. Those rounds share learning. There is no between-game variance estimate or confidence interval with one game per opponent.

Meridian beat production AISurvivor and Retcon in these games. Neither win establishes a repeated winning matchup. Retcon actually inflicted more bullet damage; Meridian's survival points mattered. Diamond repeatedly inflicted much more damage than Meridian returned, with no own skipped turns to explain the deficit. Five hits out of 131 shots and 198.55 energy spent show how little that firing bought. The data do not identify a single causal defect. The all-target promotion rule failed, so the nine confirmation games remain unspent. No confirmed winning objective is claimed; keep the runnable bot and evidence for study.
