## Why acquisition stopped

Both jobs used the required Ubuntu 26.04 label and reported Temurin 25.0.5+8. The source build ran on runner image release 20261025.179 and resolved Python 3.13.17. The measurement job ran on release 20261101.186, where the workflow's `python-version: '3.13'` selected 3.13.18. That differs from the frozen 3.13.17 fingerprint and suffices to explain the fail-closed result. The failed job did not export a complete fingerprint of its different runtime, so no claim is made about further binary differences. The measurement guard ran exactly once. The original attempt stays invalid, with no tuning, repeat, confirmation or transfer.
