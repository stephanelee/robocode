# Long-range melee firepower

[Prospective registration, issue #145](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/issues/145)

Test one melee-only change from accepted KeelHelm 0.2.1. At legal scan range ≥500 units and own energy ≥35, raise the nominal range-band power from 1.0 to 1.4, retaining the existing enemy kill cap, legal limits, reserve, circular intercept, target, radar and movement. All other ranges, low energy and duels use the accepted rule. Motivation: 3,248 of the 4,042 historical melee shots were long-range; a change in this band has measurable exposure. No tuning or alternate-fire rule.

Evaluate both policies on every live melee decision in all 12 original #138 candidate recordings / 60 rounds. Crossfire issued zero alternate requests there. Calibrate the ordinary arm against the original 4,042 shot events, 663 hits and 3,071.9068 capped damage. Use actual own scans through each tick, own pose and fixed opponent trajectories. Recompute circular lead with each selected power, pre-rotation fire ray, compensated ±20° turn, readiness and complete subsequent cooldown. Allow genuinely new and displaced shots. Carry per-round energy differences from firing costs and confirmed contact bonuses, with unrelated environmental energy changes frozen. Suppress future decisions if counterfactual energy becomes negative. Exclude duel decisions but resolve in-flight melee bullets to terminal contact/round end. Reuse pinned server segment/radius/wall and bullet intersection functions. Remove original own melee bullets from the counterfactual collision field; simulate own changed bullets together, use all other observed trajectories, with counterfactual bullet collisions removing the involved trajectories thereafter. Opponent responses and unobserved continuation of destroyed bullets are unknown. Record that fixed-field limitation.

Primary replay endpoint: sum per-tick, per-actual-victim energy-capped contact damage. Available pool for the shooter is original credited melee damage to that victim on this tick plus their remaining nonnegative observed energy; if original melee damage was already capped by overkill, use just that original credit. Changed hits can earn at most the pool. This is a fixed-trajectory approximation, calibrate baseline exactly. Report raw damage, energy cost, contacts, shot count, per-game deltas and actual victims (including Diamond). Gate is strictly positive aggregate capped damage with valid baseline; otherwise stop. No new games during this stage. A pass triggers fresh serial minimind control/candidate duels against pinned Diamond Port and four-way melee against Diamond, Prism and Keel, with prospective live protocol, frozen inputs, adequate headroom and separate implementation review before play. Zero historical scores substituted for live results. No merge.
