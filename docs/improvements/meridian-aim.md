# Meridian v3 prospective targeting experiment

V2 landed 7 of 228 shots against experimental Python Diamond Port. Hypothesis: after firing, a later legal scan shows where the opponent actually crossed the outgoing bullet front. For recent confirmed launches with similar observed lateral velocity, radial velocity, distance to a wall along the target's travel direction and flight time, these offsets may form a better aim direction than extrapolating a stop, line or constant turn. Keep v2’s defense, two movement paths, radar, firing-power rule, energy guard and alignment tolerance. Fall back to its existing gun until sufficiently supported. The new gun may change which shots align and therefore the opponent’s subsequent actions.

**Screen.** Four separate cold five-round games, in this order: exact v2 control vs pinned experimental Python Diamond Port, v3 vs Diamond Port, v3 vs Retcon and v3 vs current production AISurvivor TRAIN. Compare v3 vs fresh v2 against Diamond on uncapped bullet damage dealt per 100 confirmed shots and official score share. Require both strictly higher, every screen game valid with zero Meridian skips and all three candidate matchups separately above 35% score share with at least one official round win. A zero own-shot denominator fails the screen. Report own and enemy shots/hits, damage, energy spent, contacts and skips, plus learned vs fallback fire commands and label counts. Record every loss and invalid attempt. Engine starts are uncontrolled, so the comparison is descriptive.

**Confirmation.** Only if the entire screen passes, play three fresh twenty-round games per opponent. Each opponent requires valid games with zero own skips, mean official share above 50%, at least two games above 50% and more than 30/60 official wins. Fixed budget 20 + conditional 180 rounds. No reruns, tuning on confirmation, threshold adjustment or pooling with previous candidates. Publish IDs, seeds, isolated ports, cold paths, exact source/JAR/opponent/runtime fingerprints and independent review before competitive execution.
