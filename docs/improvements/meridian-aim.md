# Meridian v3 prospective targeting experiment

V2 landed 7 of 228 shots against experimental Python Diamond Port. Hypothesis: after firing, a later legal scan shows where the opponent actually crossed the outgoing bullet front. For recent confirmed launches with similar observed lateral velocity, radial velocity, distance to a wall along the target's travel direction and flight time, these offsets may form a better aim direction than extrapolating a stop, line or constant turn. Keep v2’s defense, two movement paths, radar, firing-power rule, energy guard and alignment tolerance. Fall back to its existing gun until sufficiently supported. The new gun may change which shots align and therefore the opponent’s subsequent actions.

**Screen.** Four separate cold five-round games, in this order: exact v2 control vs pinned experimental Python Diamond Port, v3 vs Diamond Port, v3 vs Retcon and v3 vs current production AISurvivor TRAIN. Compare v3 vs fresh v2 against Diamond on uncapped bullet damage dealt per 100 confirmed shots and official score share. Require both strictly higher, every screen game valid with zero Meridian skips and all three candidate matchups separately above 35% score share with at least one official round win. A zero own-shot denominator fails the screen. Report own and enemy shots/hits, damage, energy spent, contacts and skips, plus learned vs fallback fire commands and label counts. Record every loss and invalid attempt. Engine starts are uncontrolled, so the comparison is descriptive.

**Confirmation.** Only if the entire screen passes, play three fresh twenty-round games per opponent. Each opponent requires valid games with zero own skips, mean official share above 50%, at least two games above 50% and more than 30/60 official wins. Fixed budget 20 + conditional 180 rounds. No reruns, tuning on confirmation, threshold adjustment or pooling with previous candidates. Publish IDs, seeds, isolated ports, cold paths, exact source/JAR/opponent/runtime fingerprints and independent review before competitive execution.

[Issue #80](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/issues/80) and the [manifest](../../benchmarks/meridian-aim/games.json) register the four screen games and nine conditional games with IDs, bot seeds and separate loopback ports 31820–31832. Each ID uses a fresh build/meridian-staging/ID and build/meridian/ID. The evidence directory holds attempt.json with command and port, the official recording, runner log and private markers. AIS additionally gets fresh ais-model.dat and ais.jsonl. The v2 control's four runnable assets come from PR #79 commit 85cccdeb5f9bde8af42f971510e13f0e089f28ec verified against its profile. The [v3 profile](../../benchmarks/meridian-aim/profile.json) fixes source, JAR, runners, opponent loaders, packages and runtime hashes for the single execution.

**Label and query.** A fire request captures the current legal target state. Its turn, gun power and own center must agree with the subsequent confirmed bullet creation. On two consecutive scans bracketing the first passage of the front's far edge, measure the target bearing on the latter scan relative to its launch bearing. Orient by the sign of lateral velocity and scale by the power-dependent escape angle. This is a virtual continuation of a confirmed launch, including bullets intercepted before arrival. Samples do not require a hit. Missed scan brackets and offsets outside ±1.25 are discarded. Clear unresolved launches each round. The last 192 resolved examples persist within one game alongside v2's gun ratings and incoming hit distribution.

For the query context, compare absolute lateral speed, signed radial speed, forward wall clearance and flight time to each example. The squared inverse-distance weights vote across 51 bearings with kernel width set by the target radius at query range. At least eight completed examples and four units of effective weight are required to override the v2 forecast. Both the fresh-scan firing gate and gun-heat check still apply. Private markers count learned and fallback fire requests and scan-derived labels, while the official observer supplies actual creations, hits and damage. These counts distinguish whether the candidate used the learned gun from whether it produced useful shots.

Every game requires the exact runtime profile, two expected participants and versions, complete official rounds and all contiguous ticks, both private lifecycle markers per round and a complete cold AIS endpoint where applicable. Zero enemy shots invalidates the incoming damage metric. Zero own shots fails the screen. A single arm/opponent game cannot estimate between-game variability.

The zero-game [hosted preflight](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/actions/runs/41737978269) reproduced every asset, executable, module, wheel, interpreter version and OS field, but reported Ubuntu image revision 20261026.180.1 instead of 20261101.186.1. Before execution, register both complete byte-exact profiles. The [additional preflight fingerprint](../../benchmarks/meridian-aim/profile-20261026.json) differs in that single field. Both arms must run in the same job under the same profile. Any third fingerprint aborts before launching a game. The runner publishes its actual profile before battle.
