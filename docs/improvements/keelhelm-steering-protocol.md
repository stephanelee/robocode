Reuse the calibrated #148 control and causal trigger with t divisible by 32 instead of 16: melee, |speed|=8, |prior turn rate|<2°, wall clearance ≥80, nearest fresh own scan (age 0..3) at least 500, with at least one. Eight steering commands, target speed stays signed 8: +4.0° per tick for four ticks then −4.0° for four, multiplied by a fixed sign chosen so the first turn of the travel direction points away from the nearest fresh scan (cross-product; equal range lowest ID, collinear choose positive). At full speed the native body limit is 4°. Thus restore initial heading with lateral displacement, keeping speed. No additional trigger/parameter search and no actual bullet, observed death or private enemy state used to steer. Disjoint 24-tick windows with eight intervention ticks plus 16 follow-through ticks. During follow-through apply the contemporaneous original legal movement controller's reconstructed commands to the altered pose (frozen commands; these are not a prediction of a live controller's replanning). Restore original state between windows. Verify original pose and incoming physical contact IDs each assessed tick. Score all live incoming rays and new fires, no protective candidate bullets; retain independent wall, other-body and observed enemy-enemy terminals where applicable. Track original and hypothetical first contact tick by ID, original short-window hits avoided then hit late, introduced hits, genuine passes/independent terminals, unresolved and latent exposure. Give no savings for a surviving avoided ray until it is provably past (>18 projected along ray behind its advanced center), or independently terminated; charge all other avoided rays. Retain #148 pessimistic four-tick newly reachable envelope charges. Mask on death/melee exit/end or possible candidate ram; truncated windows allow no positive saving per shooter/range stratum. On candidate wall contact charge native damage using updated attempted speed and truncate with no positive saving. Detect/charge possible candidate ram conservatively. Report all windows, phases and nominal damage by shooter including Diamond long-range. Also report bullet IDs for the primary endpoint, without transport/session data.

Fixed gate: ≥20 original Diamond long-range hits, conservative Diamond long-range damage at least 5% lower, conservative all-shooter nominal damage plus collision penalties at least 2% lower, positive aggregate savings in ≥7 of 12 games, and no new wall/ram contacts. Require exact original trajectory/contact calibration and independent code review before scoring. Fixed-path opportunity test, no competitive claim.
