## Hypothesis and exact intervention

A modest power increase in sufficiently successful melee contexts can increase damage without spending the energy reserve. Use actual confirmed own shot outcomes, not virtual accuracy or replay/observer data. The circular aim algorithm, movement, radar, target selection, firing tolerance/energy gates, and inherited duel policy remain unchanged. Changing power naturally changes circular bullet-speed prediction. No reduced-power or other controller intervention is included.

Maintain a FIFO window of the last 32 resolved confirmed-melee-shot outcomes separately for each intended opponent ID and firing-range bin: `[0,250)`, `[250,500)`, `[500,infinity)`. Models persist across rounds only within the same cold process/game. A success is at least one positive-damage legal BulletHitBot callback for that own physical bullet (any enemy victim; collateral damage counts). Other fully resolved terminal outcomes are unsuccessful. This empirical outcome filter is not a calibrated hit probability; censoring and context selection limit its interpretation.

Select the inherited baseline `MeleePolicy.power(distance, ownEnergy, targetEnergy)` first. Increase it only when ALL hold: range <500; scan age in 0..4; own energy >=45; target energy >=8; relevant window contains 32 resolved outcomes; and `(successes+2)/(n+8) >=0.30`, evaluated exactly as `10*(successes+2) >=3*(n+8)` (with n=32, at least10 successes). The finite pseudocount is a fixed shrinkage filter, not a confidence bound. Proposed power is `min(2.6, baseline+0.4, inherited target kill cap)`, where the kill cap remains `targetEnergy<=4 ? targetEnergy/4+0.05 : (targetEnergy+2)/6+0.05`. Use it only if it exceeds baseline by at least0.2; otherwise use the exact baseline. No power can exceed2.6 or the old kill cap. An eligible request leaves at least42.4 energy before other game events. Never tune this rule from trial scores.

## Physical feedback and bounded diagnostics

Register pending metadata only after setFire succeeds. Confirm by own legal BulletFired turn (request+1), ID and selected power. Only these shots enter the bounded256-entry ledger. Eviction and round-end unresolved shots are censored, never unsuccessful training examples. Unconfirmed pending state clears on round/duel transition; confirmed shots and calibration windows survive the transition for legal resolution. From-start duels have no melee training or power intervention.

The pinned engine can detect/apply collision and bot-hit lists independently for the same bullet in one tick (issue #129 engine evidence, embedded server SHA256 `16c277795bd823c9eda0b650f1d09a418ff35cfe43d59b820c52d5e6816f905c`). Therefore collect hit/wall/collision callbacks without immediately treating the first kind as the outcome. On the next live main-loop tick, resolve shots whose terminal callbacks are from earlier ticks; a positive hit dominates all same-tick non-hit kinds. Advance this ledger before the melee/duel branch. At round end, known positive hits may resolve successfully; non-hit pending terminal records remain censored if no later live tick established delivery completion. New round clears the ledger/counters but retains only resolved windows. No synthetic expiry miss or observer information is used.

Keep a bounded4096-record per-round chronological diagnostic event log. Global event sequence persists within the game. F records contain confirmed physical identity/turn/power plus request turn, request-context event sequence, intended target/range-bin, baseline/selected power, range, own/target energy, scan age and pre-request window n/successes. R records contain resolution identity, target/bin, source callback turn, resolution turn/end marker, observed kind mask, damage and success. E records identify censored ledger evictions. Every F/R/E increments the sequence; the request-context sequence lets an independent offline replay recover the exact earlier window state. Round-end censor counts cover outstanding ledger entries; no censor event updates a window. Overflow invalidates evidence. Per-round counters and per-ID materially changed confirmed-power counts never alter decisions.
