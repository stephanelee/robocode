# Melee speed dip and survival

[Descriptive registration](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/issues/147) · [Movement rule registered before scoring](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/issues/147#issuecomment-6990902282)

Before new measurement: use exactly the 12 immutable Crossfire 0.1.0 candidate recordings / 60 rounds from #138 (zero alternate requests). Compare actual received bullet hit events and distinct bullets, nominal damage and energy-capped credit, wall and ram contacts, victim prior movement/turn, speed, nearest wall and opponent distance, shooter including Diamond, and fire vs impact phase. Separate own live melee and last-two denominators and terminal/unknown contexts. Preserve original evidence and hashes. Physical rays and observer positions serve as labels only. Register one bounded movement change and paired offline proxy/gates after description and before scoring. Require independent exact-code review and baseline calibration before proposed outcomes. No parameter search. A pass alone permits separate implementation, freeze and serial native fresh Diamond 1v1 and top-four melee plus transfer checks. No rerun of past experiments or merge.

Descriptive stage: 547 incoming melee-impact bullet events, 1,920.22 capped damage over 59,825 live decisions. Diamond accounted for 1,038.78. 452 hits originated at ≥500 units (1,654.6 credit). Full-speed ticks took 1,618.8 damage on 47,012 ticks; open area (clearance ≥80) took 1,081.5 on 27,046. No observed own wall or ram contact in melee. These rates motivate testing whether a short speed change breaks long-range leads; they do not establish causation.

One prospective movement-only test: at t divisible by 16, when in melee, |speed|=8, |last body turn rate|<2°, body clearance ≥80, and the nearest of all own scans age 0–3 is ≥500 (at least one), briefly reduce speed in the current direction. Hold zero turn rate for eight ticks, signed target speed 4 for the first four and 8 for the next four. Then release to ordinary movement. No bullets, enemy fire/death estimates, opponent identities/private knowledge or future events in the trigger. Reset scan state each round, update from candidate-owned scans, age out old sightings. Do not tune. Hypothesis: an opponent firing a long-range lead at a straight, full-speed bot overshoots during the speed dip.

Test all triggered nonoverlapping eight-tick windows, each anchored to the original state. Baseline uses the actual accepted movement selector and native-calibrated one-step physics; require predicted original command endpoints to match observed poses on eligible window ticks, or stop and explain. Calibrate native shot segment vs radius18 to actual incoming hit IDs per window tick. Score primary incoming nominal damage at first contact per enemy bullet, plus native collision costs. Use all already-live/newly-fired historical enemy rays as labels only; a surviving ray continues analytically after it hit the original bot or its own bullet shield, with recorded other-bot, wall and enemy-bullet terminals applied as available. Future movement, firing and scan timing outside each window remain unknown. No new protection credited from candidate bullets. Mask at observed melee exit, death or round end; report incomplete windows, permit no positive savings in truncated windows. Charge any avoided original hit whose ray is still ahead of the candidate at end unless it has terminated independently or its advanced position is beyond the candidate by >18 along the firing direction. Also charge each surviving enemy ray reachable by the candidate body within the next four ticks but unreachable by the original from its endpoint (reachable disk radius 18+8*k at lookahead tick k). This is a pessimistic bound, not observed damage. Evaluate wall and ram contact: with ≥80 starting clearance no wall reachable in 8 ticks; on possible candidate ram (≤36 center distance against fixed opponent path) censor the remainder, retain observed losses, add 0.6 contact penalty and allow no positive saving for that window. Count masks and ties. Explicitly report paired gains/losses, impact damage by shooter, raw before conservative charges, and latent charges. No reweighting or treating windows as independent rounds. Require ≥30 original hit events on evaluated windows, conservative aggregate damage reduction ≥5%, strictly positive savings in ≥7/12 games, no increase in Diamond damage after conservative charges, and no additional wall or ram contact. Independent pre-score review and reproduction, no live games unless all gates pass.
