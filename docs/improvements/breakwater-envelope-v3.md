# Breakwater 0.3 reachable-path gun

## Hypothesis

Against pinned Diamond Port, Breakwater 0.2 hit 10 of 603 shots, spent 652.49 energy firing and dealt 59.96 damage. Linear, stopped and circular predictions each earned a single virtual hit in the last 32 jointly resolved cohorts. Against AISurvivor, v0.2 won five rounds but hit only 16 of 1,289 shots. These records motivate a change in aiming and firing selectivity while retaining the v0.2 movement and shot-power policies. [Previous report](breakwater-mobile-v2-exact-results.md)

Diamond's pinned mover evaluates clockwise, counterclockwise and stop options. Our gun will project three possible trajectories from legal scans using the Tank Royale motion and wall constraints. It will aim where the most projected target footprints overlap when a bullet could arrive. Agreement of at least two paths makes a shot eligible. Otherwise it will hold fire and reconsider next turn. The three paths are hypotheses about the opponent; Diamond's choice and internal models are unavailable. [Source analysis](../opponents/diamond-port.md)

## Gun and diagnostic definitions

Treat the current firing position as the fixed orbit center. Initialize target pose from the last legal scan. Project continuing and reversing the observed lateral sign (positive default at zero) and braking to target speed zero. Moving paths use speed 8, back-as-front steering, the pinned engine step and arena constraints, a 650 px orbit-distance preference and a 160 px wall probe rotated in 5° increments. Search up to 90 ticks. The target is an 18 px radius circle. Intersect each trajectory's circle with the appropriate same-tick bullet travel annulus. For each proposed angle, count each distinct path at most once, even if its footprint spans several ticks. Evaluate interval endpoints and midpoints, maximize distinct paths covered, then prefer wider sectors, less displacement from direct aim, and finally the smaller normalized angle. These are equal-weight movement hypotheses.

A common opportunity has a scan no older than one tick, a cool gun and enough energy for v0.2's power. The candidate can fire only if its proposed ray covers at least two paths, the actual gun heading covers at least two, and heading error meets the unchanged v0.2 tolerance. Distinguish a supported proposed ray from the physically eligible shot. Shadow logs both angles and the current-heading ray, as well as round/command/creation turns, geometry and the eligibility flags. Contiguous following scans jointly score all three rays against a radius-18 target until the bullet has passed. Discard an unresolved label on a scan gap, horizon limit or round end. The runner replays the labels from the raw scans and reconciles every opportunity to its sealed round. The competitive games start cold, with the reachable-path gun controlling fire and no diagnostic stream feeding decisions.
