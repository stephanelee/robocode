# Melee damage attribution and long-range shot test

[Descriptive registration](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/issues/143) · [Intervention registered after attribution and before measurement](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/issues/143#issuecomment-6989964678)

Attribution (original 12 games, before testing any remedy): official bullet damage Crossfire 3,434 vs Diamond 4,150. Melee-at-fire scored credit 3,071.91 vs 3,819.58, 663/4,042 vs 770/4,148 bullets hitting a bot. Last-two scored credit 360.72 vs 331.97. On 3,248 Crossfire melee shots at scan range ≥500, only 484 hit their intended opponent. Retain all 4,042 melee shots in the paired test. All 240 cumulative round/owner damage figures agree with energy-capped event credit within scoreboard rounding. Opponent behavior and aim quality are not identified causal explanations.

Register ONE remedy: change the far-range (latest legal scan distance ≥500) circular intercept stopping rule to place the target at the midpoint of the engine’s current-to-next bullet segment on each future tick: first f≥1 satisfying (f+0.5)*bulletSpeed >= distance(predictedPose[f], self), cap 120, retaining the original scan-age extrapolation, wall physics, target, power, geometry and angular tolerance. All shorter ranges and duels use ordinary aim. No parameter search. Primary motivation is the physical hit test one segment ahead after bullet advancement. Propagate a compensated shadow gun (±20°) from round start on actual melee turns from own scans only. On each original physical slot, use its physical origin, time and power; evaluate its prior-command shadow ray only if the unchanged target passes the ordinary fire gate on this ray, else count as suppressed. No filling gaps or early shots. No speculative hit energy. Timing/trajectory are fixed and the result is a conditional opportunity check only.

Pre-score geometry calibration: on every original melee shot, compare earliest physical wall, bullet collision (both bullet operands), bot hit victim set (all on that tick), or round-end censor to actual terminal kinds/turn/victims; require exact agreement for all 4,042 and all 620 intended plus 43 collateral hit events. Same-tick bullet and body contacts coexist. Use engine 1.4.0 wall-first, post-advance current→next segment, current centers/radius18, actual recorded tracks of other bullets, including same-tick terminated bullets. Evaluate shifted ray as one changed shot at a time against those fixed paths. If its real bullet collided but changed ray survives that original collision tick, censor further travel there since the other bullet's later trajectory is unobserved. Keep round-end censoring and suppression, no credit for unknowns; baseline positives still count as lost. Future opponent positions only label outcomes. Report observed vs projected intended and collateral contacts, paired gains/losses, exposure, per-game and per-intended-opponent counts. Gate requires exact baseline, net ≥20 intended-hit shots across the 4,042 opportunities, strictly positive net in ≥7/12 games, nonnegative net separately for Diamond, Prism and Keel intended, and no loss in the number of shots contacting any bot. This is a screening threshold, not a p-value. Failure ends this route. Independent pre-score and output review.
