# KeelHelm Crossfire prospective comparison

Registered before implementation or play in [issue #138](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/issues/138). Parent commit `f566887afa693b45a2c113e02584302c475d88e1`, accepted KeelHelm 0.2.1 JAR SHA256 `19f779d4c4df97e9510f22bc561c0b19af0c07eaa1f1f3fd1d93f70992f7e55b`.

## Hypothesis

KeelHelm 0.2.1 led survival but trailed Diamond Port and Prism in official bullet damage in the [latest top-four snapshot](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/blob/master/docs/improvements/keelhelm-top4-latest-results.md). In the separate [retained aim diagnostic](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/blob/master/docs/improvements/keelhelm-aim-diagnostic-results.md), 7,972 of 9,952 observed shot crossings originated from scan ranges at least 500. A separate candidate tests whether firing along an already aligned gun ray at a fresh, nearer alternate opponent can add measurable damage without eroding survival. These observations motivate the test. They do not estimate extra-shot availability or prove the prior damage shortfall's cause.

## Fixed intervention

Fork accepted KeelHelm 0.2.1 as **KeelHelm Crossfire 0.1.0** in a separate project. Only in melee turns, preserve incumbent targeting, movement, radar, circular aim, power rule and next gun turn. If the gun is cool and the incumbent fails its ordinary firing predicates, consider other living scanned opponents at age 0–3 and range strictly below 500. Apply their own existing circular intercept, power and baseline energy reserve/current-ray alignment predicates. Choose the shortest current scan range then lowest legal ID. Make at most one fire request. The incumbent has priority and cannot trigger a second request through API rejection. Duels from the start and the ordinary last-survivor duel policy use the accepted commands. Track alternate requests and separately bind physical fire confirmations when the callback and command agree in round, turn and power. Record mismatches as unknown. Intended alternate is not hit attribution.

Use the accepted prior roster and existing audited acquisition tools. Four-player melee comprises 32 fresh blocked layouts × 5 rounds per arm, each with one KeelHelm arm, Keel 0.1.0, Prism 0.2.0 and pinned Diamond Port 1.8.28. Freeze fresh IDs, geometry, isolated bot seeds, exact opponent artifacts, engine/runner/JDK/Python, unchanged limits and the assignment. Native opponent randomness and scan ordering remain live. Every game begins with cold process/state. No use of already spent games or separate tuning budget.

All gates are required. Diamond duel candidate mean share >50%, official first places >20/40, >50% share in at least 3/4 games and mean candidate-minus-control share ≥−5 percentage points. Retcon candidate mean share >50% and each of Retcon/Walls/SpinBot mean candidate-minus-control share ≥−5 points. Melee candidate mean share must exceed Diamond's mean share and candidate must individually outscore Diamond in ≥17/32 games, lead aggregate official score and take unique first by score in ≥17/32 games. Candidate-minus-control melee mean share must be ≥+2 points and pass the one-sided exact balanced-assignment test at p≤0.05. Record ties, all scores/wins, damage, energy, request/physical counts and unknowns. These are fixed engineering gates for this roster and runtime.

No replacement, early score stop, extra confirmation, adaptation to outcomes, automatic promotion, or retry.
