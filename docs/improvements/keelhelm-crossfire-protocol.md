## Hypothesis

KeelHelm 0.2.1 led survival but trailed Diamond Port and Prism in official bullet damage in the [latest top-four snapshot](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/blob/master/docs/improvements/keelhelm-top4-latest-results.md). A separate candidate tests whether firing along an already aligned gun ray at a fresh, nearer alternate opponent can add measurable damage without eroding survival. These observations motivate the test. They do not estimate extra-shot availability or prove the prior damage shortfall's cause.
