## Findings supported by source

**A shadow surfer is feasible as an estimate, not an exact reconstruction.** Retcon starts its 47-bin histogram with a head-on prior, updates a kernel around matched hit bins, and chooses between predicted clockwise/counterclockwise trajectories. But the update depends on Retcon's own scan sequence, detected waves, wave matching, previous self state, and persistent lateral-direction state. The server sends a scan event privately to the scanning bot; AIS does not receive Retcon's scans. Knowing initialization and rules does not recover those hidden observations. An offline oracle using complete state can test an implementation, but cannot establish live reconstruction fidelity. See [WaveSurfMovement, lines 31–140](https://github.com/robocode-dev/rumble-bots/blob/28981c7a469ac064194b41001cd7183ec5ecf189/bots/java/Retcon/src/fnl/bots/retcon/movement/WaveSurfMovement.java#L31-L140), [EnemyFireSense, lines 90–228](https://github.com/robocode-dev/rumble-bots/blob/28981c7a469ac064194b41001cd7183ec5ecf189/bots/java/Retcon/src/fnl/bots/framework/stages/EnemyFireSense.java#L90-L228), and [private scan delivery](https://github.com/robocode-dev/tank-royale/blob/v1.4.0/server/src/main/kotlin/dev/robocode/tankroyale/server/core/TurnProcessor.kt#L323-L335).

**Fallback random reversals are conditionally suppressed, not proven absent.** With a fresh anchored unmatched wave, its radius at the next decision is one bullet-speed step. The surfer accepts it when origin-to-self distance exceeds twice bullet speed: roughly 22–39.4 units for legal powers. That supports rarity of the fire-triggered fallback at ordinary ranges. It does not quantify exceptions involving close combat, rejected/matched/missing waves, or observation state. Wall reversals remain independent. Knowing seed 1 also requires knowing the complete RNG invocation history, including draws that precede a cooldown rejection. See [wave eligibility](https://github.com/robocode-dev/rumble-bots/blob/28981c7a469ac064194b41001cd7183ec5ecf189/bots/java/Retcon/src/fnl/bots/retcon/movement/WaveSurfMovement.java#L53-L110) and [OrbitMovement](https://github.com/robocode-dev/rumble-bots/blob/28981c7a469ac064194b41001cd7183ec5ecf189/bots/java/Retcon/src/fnl/bots/retcon/movement/OrbitMovement.java#L35-L117).

**Dual-model dodging should use uncertain aim corridors and swept trajectories.** Retcon fires along its current gun heading before rotation. Its requested model angle need only be within `atan(18 / distance)` of that heading; at distance 450 the tolerance is about 2.29 degrees. Enemy scans provide body direction, not gun/radar headings. The two requested aim models therefore do not identify two exact live bullet rays. Scan age, firing time, power, and motion also add uncertainty. Evaluate clearance throughout each candidate trajectory, not only at its endpoint. Virtual paired aim tests are useful mechanism diagnostics, but do not prove live advantage: successful candidate hits change Retcon's energy, movement, and future observations. See [Gun, especially corrected alignment anchor 57–61](https://github.com/robocode-dev/rumble-bots/blob/28981c7a469ac064194b41001cd7183ec5ecf189/bots/java/Retcon/src/fnl/bots/framework/roles/Gun.java#L14-L67) and [ScannedBotEvent fields](https://github.com/robocode-dev/tank-royale/blob/v1.4.0/bot-api/java/src/main/java/dev/robocode/tankroyale/botapi/events/ScannedBotEvent.java).

**Power does not uniquely identify range.** Retcon's distance multiplier is combined with its own low-energy multiplier and a target-energy finishing cap. Power above 1.2 provides a one-way bound under this policy—fire-time range below 400—but low power can arise at several ranges. An actually inferred shot power is preferable to recomputing power from a current scan, because energy and distance may have changed since firing. Heat and wall correction are useful separate hypotheses; Retcon itself guesses pre-impact speed for wall damage. See [BasicFirePower](https://github.com/robocode-dev/rumble-bots/blob/28981c7a469ac064194b41001cd7183ec5ecf189/bots/java/Retcon/src/fnl/bots/retcon/firepower/BasicFirePower.java#L19-L32) and [wall estimate](https://github.com/robocode-dev/rumble-bots/blob/28981c7a469ac064194b41001cd7183ec5ecf189/bots/java/Retcon/src/fnl/bots/framework/stages/EnemyFireSense.java#L96-L104).

**The GF explanation in A7 is incorrect.** AIS updates its targeting histogram when a virtual targeting wave reaches the observed target, independently of an actual bullet hit. Cold/fixed evaluation freezes these updates. Consequently the claimed feedback loop of reinforcing actual hit bins cannot explain the cold baseline's hit rate. The underlying aiming policy can still be tested independently. See [AIS virtual-wave update and training guard](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/blob/b8d63ef49c1679db1893fd722c1306adfe9022be/src/main/java/AISurvivor.java#L316-L345).

Native aggregate counters can reveal discrepancies, but cannot alone classify phantom/missed waves or timing defects: errors can cancel, and completed-wave observation lifetimes differ. The retained native export contains cumulative final counters, not a shot-by-shot event history. Such classification requires aligned offline event/intent traces or appropriate retained snapshots. Retcon exports must remain offline diagnostics, never private-state input to AIS during play.

## Numerical checks and limits

The six old-code comparison shares reproduce a mean of 12.6149% and sample SD of 3.4766 percentage points. An independent Python simulation with seed 20261102 and 200,000 draws per effect reproduces the review's **score-only conditional model**: three independent game differences, each normal with SD `sqrt(2) * 3.48`.

| True improvement, pp | Tactical score guards pass | Correctness score guards pass |

| 0 | 3.89% | 36.13% |
| 2 | 14.56% | 61.16% |
| 5 | 49.62% | 88.60% |

These are not measured full-gate acceptance probabilities. Wins, skips, and wall guards are omitted; the SD comes from six cold-evaluation games rather than the fixed/online arms; normality and independence are assumptions; cross-arm/game correlation is unknown. Squaring 0.36 assumes independent vetoes and is hypothetical. The six-games-per-arm claim also needs an explicit estimand: with a +5 pp effect floor and true +7 pp, the mean-score rule alone passes about 84% under this model; additional guards reduce acceptance. This supports prospective budget calibration, not retrospective relaxation of the registered experiment.

The trigonometric fixtures reproduce: `500*sin(9°)=78.217`, `500*sin(2°)=17.450`, `100*sin(9°)=15.643`; the stated constant-heading first-wall fixture reaches `(782,312)`; the illustrative arrival times are `450/11=40.909` and `17+450/19.7=39.843`. They validate their simplified geometries, not an observed battle effect.

One travel-time interpretation needs correction: `450/16.4=27.439` is head-on travel time, not a bound on Retcon's iterative linear aim. With perpendicular target speed 8, no wall clipping, and initial distance 450, its model first intercepts at step 32, with lateral displacement 256. Thus approximately 216 units is not an upper bound on the separation of its model rays. See [LinearAimModel iteration](https://github.com/robocode-dev/rumble-bots/blob/28981c7a469ac064194b41001cd7183ec5ecf189/bots/java/Retcon/src/fnl/bots/retcon/gun/LinearAimModel.java#L21-L39).

## Proposed prospective roadmap edits

1. Preserve the current foundation preregistration and report its outcome under its existing gates.
2. Prioritize a dual-model corridor feasibility diagnostic, including legal observation age, current-versus-requested heading, inferred shot power, wall motion, and full swept clearance. Register a separate live trial before measuring strength.
3. Bring a shadow-surfer fidelity diagnostic forward. Compare legal-information estimates with offline truth, exposing scan gaps, wave matching ambiguity, histogram error, and direction prediction; do not label it exact mirroring. Keep oracle and legal-information results separate.
4. Treat heat/wall inference and distance/power exposure as independent hypotheses with event-level diagnostics. Aggregate Retcon counters cannot establish AIS defect frequency or causality.
5. Prospectively calibrate later budgets and effect thresholds using uncertainty and the entire decision rule; publish assumptions, rejected candidates, and failures. Do not reuse these conditional simulation rates as empirical full-gate power.
