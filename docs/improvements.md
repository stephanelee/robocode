| A/A Retcon noise calibration / [PR #37](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/pull/37) | **No gameplay change; proposal only.** 400 fixed minimum-risk rounds, identical bundle in both arms (10 blocks Ã— 20 per arm). The block-delta SD is 5.586 pp. **Review correction:** 8 blocks gives about 81% *significance-only* power at +5 pp, but the proposed rule with a +5 pp observed floor accepts a true +5 pp only about 49% of the time, at any budget.
