# Measured AISurvivor improvements

[Tracker: three measured cycles](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/issues/8)

Earlier cycles compare narrow candidates with the previously accepted version. Current work builds one coherent bot before a whole-product benchmark. Keep source commits, JAR hashes, opponent pin, runtime, round budget, AISurvivor seeds, raw telemetry, failures, and the final accept/reject decision. Do not describe short in-roster trials as proof of generalization or competitive superiority.

## Baselines

- [Original sample baseline](benchmarks/samples-2026-11-02.md): 140 rounds completed. All evaluation policies beat the easy sample roster; trained wall collisions exposed a weakness. This validates integration, not strength.
- [Retcon 1v1 baseline](benchmarks/retcon-2026-11-02.md) / [issue #7](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/issues/7): 35 rounds completed. Fixed movement won 1/5, cold DQN 0/5, trained DQN 0/5; wall collisions and damage exposure motivate movement diagnostics. No AISurvivor gameplay change is included in the integration PR.

| A/A Retcon noise calibration / [PR #37](https://github.com/stephanelee/robocode/pull/37) | **No gameplay change; proposal only.** 400 fixed minimum-risk rounds, identical bundle in both arms (10 blocks × 20 per arm). The block-delta SD is 5.586 pp. **Review correction:** 8 blocks gives about 81% *significance-only* power at +5 pp, but the proposed rule with a +5 pp observed floor accepts a true +5 pp only about 49% of the time, at any budget.
